1Welcome

You are standing at a counter, there is a queue, and you need to ring up a sale. This manual is built for that moment: short steps, plain words, and a one-page Quick card you can print and stick next to the till.

The Till is the app you sell on. It runs in the browser on the shop's till, tablet or phone. It is made to keep selling when the internet drops: every sale is saved on the till first and sent to the server afterwards (see Working offline).

Who does what

Cashier
Rings up sales, takes payment, gives slips, looks up past sales and can start a refund. A refund above the shop's limit needs a manager.
Manager
Does everything a cashier does, and also: approves refunds and lower prices with their PIN, cancels a whole sale, and sees the Stock and Transfers tabs.
Owner
Sets the shop up in the back office (staff, tills, products). An owner can approve at the till in the same way a manager can.

What you see on the screen

  • The top bar. It shows Online or Working offline, how many sales are still waiting to be sent, when the till was last updated, an Update now button and a language list.
  • The greeting. After you unlock, Hello, Thandi (your own name) and a Lock till button.
  • The tabs. Sell is always first. The other tabs depend on your role and on what the owner switched on for the shop. A tab that needs the internet is greyed out while the till is offline.

This manual describes what the app does today. Part of the app is still being finished: real printers, cash drawers and scales are not connected yet, and WhatsApp, SMS and email slips are not sent from the till screen. Where that matters, the chapter says so plainly.

2Setting up the till

A new till has to be told which shop it belongs to, and the owner has to approve it. You do this once. Cashiers normally never see this screen again.

What the owner gives you

In the back office, under Staff and tills, the owner registers the till, then chooses Show setup. The owner gets three things and must give them to whoever sets up the till:

  • the shop number (a long code with dashes),
  • the till number (also a long code with dashes), and
  • the setup code.

The setup code is shown to the owner only once. If it is lost, the owner must reset the till in the back office to get a new one. The sales already waiting on the till are kept.

Register this till

  1. Open the Till app in the browser. On a new till you see Register this till.
  2. Type the Shop number, the Till number and the Setup code.
  3. Press Register till. The button stays grey until all three boxes are filled.

If a number looks wrong the till tells you: The shop number does not look right. It is a long code with dashes. or The till number does not look right. It is a long code with dashes. Copy the numbers again from the owner rather than typing them from memory.

If the browser is not allowed to store data, you will see This till could not save its registration. Check that the browser is allowed to store data. Do not use a private window for the till.

Waiting for approval

The till does not check with the server the moment you register, so you can do this even without a connection. At the first update the till asks the server whether the owner approved it. Until then you will see:

This till is waiting for the owner to approve it in the back office. You can sell, but nothing is sent until it is approved.

The owner opens Staff and tills and presses Approve next to the till. The banner goes away at the next update. You can press Update now in the top bar to speed it up (it needs the internet).

If the owner switches the till off you will see The owner has switched this till off. Ask the owner.

Staff, products and promotions download by themselves

Once the till is approved and online it downloads the shop's staff, products, promotions and business settings. You do not do anything. The top bar shows Updated 08:42 (the time of the last download), or Not updated yet if it has never worked. After that the till can sell and check PINs with no internet at all.

Until the staff have downloaded, the PIN screen says No staff on this till yet. Ask the owner to add you. Connect the till to the internet once and press Update now. If it still says that, the owner has not added you in the back office.

Setting a till up again

Sometimes the server stops accepting a till, for example because its saved credential was lost. The till then shows Set this till up again with the old numbers filled in. The owner must add or reset the till in the back office (that shows a new setup code). Type the new Setup code and press Set up till again. If sales are waiting, the screen tells you: sales are waiting on this till. They are kept and will be sent once the till is set up again. Nothing is lost.

If the owner wipes the till

The owner can ask for a till to be cleared (for example if it was stolen or is being replaced). The till first sends every waiting sale. While it still has sales to send you will see The owner asked for this till to be cleared. … sales must be sent first. Keep the till online. Leave it connected until the message goes. After the wipe the till is no longer registered.

3Unlocking with your PIN

Every person has their own PIN. The till is locked until someone signs in, and it records who rang up each sale.

  1. On Who is selling? press your name.
  2. The screen says PIN for and your name. Tap your PIN on the number pad. A PIN is 4 to 6 digits. Dots show how many you have typed.
  3. Press OK. Use Delete to remove the last digit.
  4. You see Hello, and your name. You are in.

Picked the wrong name? Press Not you? to go back to the list.

Wrong PIN and the lock

A wrong PIN shows That PIN is not right. Tries left: 4. (the number goes down). After 5 wrong tries that person is locked for 5 minutes. The pad goes grey and says Too many wrong tries. Try again in 5 min. and counts down.

The lock belongs to the person, and it is saved on the till. Closing the app, switching the till off or choosing a different name does not clear it. Another cashier can still sign in with their own PIN while yours waits.

The same lock protects a manager's PIN when a manager approves a refund or a price (see Lowering a price). Guessing a manager's PIN at approval time uses up the same five tries.

Locking the till

Whenever you step away, press Lock till. The sale you were busy with stays on the screen and is still there when you unlock. Do not leave a till unlocked: whatever is sold under your name is your sale.

Never tell anyone your PIN. A manager's PIN approves refunds and price drops. Anyone who knows it can take money from the shop in your name.

4Selling

Scan or type, check the list, press Pay. That is nearly all of it.

Scan a barcode

  1. Click in the box that says Scan or type a barcode or name. It is already ready when you open the Sell tab.
  2. Scan the barcode with the scanner, or type the number and press Enter (or press Add).
  3. The item appears in the list with its price. Scan the same item again to add one more.

Try it with 6001234500011 (bread). If the till does not know a code it says We do not have that barcode. Put the item aside and tell the owner, who can add it in the back office.

Search by name

Type two or more letters of the name. Up to six matches appear, each with its price (a weighed item shows /kg). Press the one you want. If only one item matches, Enter takes it. If nothing matches you will see Nothing found for that.

Changing the list

  • + adds one more, − takes one off.
  • ✕ removes the whole line.
  • Clear sale empties everything and starts again. Anything already taken from a voucher, points or a school wallet for this sale is given back (see Vouchers, points and the school wallet).

An empty sale says Nothing in this sale yet.

Items sold by weight

Meat, mince, bulk goods: these are sold by the gram. When you pick or scan one, the screen changes to Weight of Mince in grams.

  1. Put the item on the scale and read the weight.
  2. Type the weight in grams (700 for 700 g). Whole numbers only.
  3. Press Add weight. The line shows the grams, for example 700 g, and the price for that weight.

If the weight is not allowed (zero, for example) the till says That weight is not allowed. Press Back to leave without adding.

A Read from scale button appears only when a scale is connected to the till. No real scale adapter is built yet, so on most tills you type the weight (see Printer, drawer and scale).

Some shops use price labels printed by a scale. If your shop does, scan the label and the till adds the item at the weight or price on the label. Such a line has no + and −, and its price cannot be lowered. To change it, remove the line and scan again.

Add-ons

An add-on (extra cheese, a bag, a side) is a product like any other in this app. Scan it or search for it by name and add it as another line. It carries its own price and takes its own stock. There is no separate "add-on" button on the Sell tab.

Promotions and discounts

Promotions (percent off, amount off, a fixed price, combos) are applied for you when the right items are in the sale, on the right days and hours. You do not type anything. When a promotion applies, the totals show Discount with the amount taken off.

Only a promotion or a manager-approved price (next sections) can lower a price. A cashier cannot give a discount on their own.

The totals

Discount
What promotions took off (only shown when there is one).
VAT included
The VAT that is already inside the prices.
Total
What the customer pays for the goods.
Tip / To pay
Only if a tip was added: the tip, and the final amount to pay.

Tips

If the shop takes tips, a row of buttons appears once there is something in the sale: No tip, Tip 10%, Tip 15%, and a box Tip amount with Set tip. Type an amount such as 10 or 7,50.

A tip cannot be more than the bill (A tip cannot be more than the bill.). The tip is added to what the customer pays and goes in the cash drawer, but it is not counted as a sale. A tip cannot be added to a credit sale.

Attaching a customer

If the shop uses customers and loyalty points, you can attach the sale to a customer. Type their Customer phone number and press Find customer. If several people match, pick the right one. You will see Customer: Name and a Remove customer button.

Finding a customer needs the internet: Customers can only be found when the till is online. If nobody has that number you get No customer has that phone number. New customers are added in the Customers tab (see Serial numbers, customers and appointments).

5Taking payment

Press Pay and ask how the customer is paying. The button shows the amount, for example Pay R42,00.

The screen asks How is the customer paying? and shows the total. You choose one of these: Cash, Card, QR, Credit, and, if the shop has it switched on, Split payment. Back returns to the sale without selling.

Cash

  1. Press Cash.
  2. Either press a quick button (the first is Exact; the others are notes that cover the bill, such as R50 or R100), or type the money you were given in Cash received.
  3. When the money covers the bill, the button turns into Change: R8,00. Press it.
  4. Give the change. The screen shows Sale saved. Total R42,00. Change: R8,00.

A quick button completes the sale immediately. Make sure it is the note the customer actually handed you. Pressing R100 when you were given R50 means the till will tell you to give R58 too much.

Card

Press Card after the card machine has said approved. The sale is saved at once as a card sale.

The till is not connected to the card machine. It does not know whether the payment worked. Press Card or QR only after the customer's payment has gone through on the machine or on their phone. Once saved, the sale is saved.

QR

Same as card, for a customer paying by a QR code on their phone. Press QR only after you have seen the payment arrive.

Split payments

When one customer pays in more than one way, for example R20 cash and the rest on card. Split payment appears only if the owner switched it on for the shop.

  1. Press Split payment. You get Part 1.
  2. Choose the method (Method for part 1: Cash, Card, QR, Wallet, Voucher or Points) and type the amount (Amount for part 1).
  3. For cash, fill in Cash received for part 1 if the customer gave more than that part. Leaving it empty means the exact amount.
  4. Press Add a part for the next method. It fills in the amount still to pay.
  5. Watch Still to pay: R0,00. When it is zero, press Pay.
  • A sale can be split into at most 6 payments, and each method can be used once.
  • If the parts add up to too much you see Over by R5,00. Fix an amount, or press ✕ to remove a part.
  • Credit cannot be part of a split.
  • Wallet, voucher and points parts need their reference. The till fills it in for you when you use the buttons described in the next chapter.
  • If something is wrong: Check the amounts. Wallet and voucher payments need their reference, and cash received cannot be less than the amount.

After the sale

The screen returns to an empty sale and shows Sale saved. Total R42,00. The sale is now safe on the till, whether or not there is internet. If the shop has a printer and cash drawer connected, the slip prints and the drawer opens after a cash sale (see Printer, drawer and scale).

If the printer or drawer fails the sale still stands. The till adds a plain-words note, such as The slip did not print: The printer is out of paper. Put in a new roll.

6Vouchers, points and the school wallet

These three take money from something the customer holds, not from their hand. They need the internet, because the server must confirm the balance.

Vouchers, points and wallets work only while the till is online. Offline, the buttons are disabled and the till says Vouchers, points and wallets need the internet. Connect the till to use them. A till that is not registered also cannot use them.

How to use them at the counter

They are used from the Split payment screen, even if the customer is paying the whole bill that way. Under Pay with something that needs the internet you will see the buttons the shop has switched on: Use voucher, Use points, Pay from wallet. If you have no Split payment button, ask the owner to switch it on.

A gift voucher

  1. On the split screen press Use voucher.
  2. Type or scan the Voucher code. Amount to take is filled with what is still to pay. Lower it if the voucher is worth less, or if the customer wants to use part of it.
  3. Press Take from voucher.
  4. The part is added and locked: Taken and locked in: reference …. Finish the rest of the bill with other parts, then press Pay.

Only one voucher can be used on a sale: A voucher is already used on this sale. You cannot take more than is left to pay: Type an amount more than zero and not more than what is left to pay.

Loyalty points

  1. Attach the customer to the sale first (Find customer). Without it you will see Attach the customer to the sale first, then use their points.
  2. On the split screen press Use points.
  3. Type the whole number of Points to use and press Take points.

The till checks first. The customer has only 120 points. or Those points are worth R…, more than the R… still to pay. Use fewer points. Points can be used once per sale.

School wallet at the Sell tab

  1. On the split screen press Pay from wallet.
  2. Choose How the wallet is read: QR code (the learner's phone) or Card or wristband.
  3. Scan or type the code in Wallet QR code or card number.
  4. Check Amount to take and press Take from wallet.
  5. The till says Took R15,00 from Name's wallet. Check the name and the child in front of you.

The wallet is charged once for a given sale. Pressing again after a lost answer is safe: the same sale never takes the money twice.

The School wallet tab

Schools' tuckshops also have a School wallet tab (shown when the owner has switched it on for the shop). It is a quick screen for wallet-only sales: add the items, then Scan the QR code or type it, say whether the code is from A phone (QR code) or A card or wristband, and press Take R15,00 from the wallet. For a teacher there is a tick box Charge a teacher's monthly staff account.

After a good payment you see the person's name, grade and class, Left in the wallet, and Check that the person in front of you is Name. If a photo is on file the screen says so. Look at the child. A card in the wrong hands is how wallets are stolen from.

Selling and checking vouchers

The Vouchers tab has three parts:

  • Sell a voucher. Type the Voucher value (from R1 up to R5000), choose how the customer pays (Cash, Card, QR) and press Sell voucher. The code is shown once: Give this code to the customer now. It is shown only once and cannot be looked up again. Press I have given the code to the customer only after you have done that.
  • Check a balance. Type the code and press Check balance. It says how much is left (it shows only the last four characters) and until when, or This voucher has been cancelled. or This voucher has expired.
  • Use a voucher. Takes an amount off a voucher and shows a payment reference. It is the same thing the Use voucher button does inside a sale; prefer that button.

A voucher code cannot be looked up again. If you close the screen before the customer has the code, the money is on a voucher nobody can use. Write the code down or show it on the screen before you press I have given the code to the customer.

7Credit customers

Some regular customers buy on credit and pay later. The owner sets them up in the back office, with a credit limit.

Selling on credit

  1. Build the sale and press Pay, then Credit.
  2. On Choose the credit customer press the customer's name. Next to each name you see what they already owe, for example owes R120,00.
  3. The sale is saved on their account.

If the sale takes them over their limit the till warns you: This sale takes Name over their credit limit. You can still go ahead. The decision is yours. Follow what the shop owner told you.

If there are no credit customers you see There are no credit customers on this till yet. The owner adds them in the back office. A sale with a tip cannot go on credit: Take the tip off to sell on credit.

When a credit customer pays

  1. Open the Credit book tab.
  2. Find the customer (Find a customer by name or phone) and press Take payment.
  3. You see Name owes R120,00. Type the Amount paid and choose Paid by (Cash, Card or QR).
  4. Press Save payment. It says Payment of R50,00 from Name saved. They now owe R70,00.

A payment cannot be larger than what the customer owes: That is more than Name owes (R120,00).

The credit book works offline. It then shows the balances from the last time it was online, plus what has been taken since. Cash taken here counts in the end-of-day cash-up as Paid on credit accounts in cash.

8Slips

The shop does not print a slip for every sale. Give one only if the customer asks. That saves paper and money.

The QR slip

Right after a sale, the sale screen shows a Get QR slip button. Press it, and the till shows QR slip: and a web address. The customer can scan or open it on their phone. A QR slip is free to give. The public slip page does not show staff or customer details.

It can say:

  • The QR slip will be available once this sale has been sent to the server. The sale is still waiting on the till. Wait a moment and press again.
  • Connect the till to the internet to get the QR slip. You are offline.
  • This till is not registered, so it cannot get a QR slip.

If a printer is connected, Print slip with QR prints the slip with its QR code on it.

WhatsApp, SMS and email slips

The server can send a slip by WhatsApp, SMS or email, with at most 3 slips per sale. But these three are not yet offered on the Till screen: only Get QR slip is. The message providers are also not yet connected for real sending. Until this changes, the customer takes a QR slip or a printed slip.

On a paid plan, slips by WhatsApp or SMS are counted against the shop's monthly message bundle. If a free plan's bundle runs out, the slip turns into a QR code.

9Lowering a price

A cashier cannot lower a price alone. A manager (or the owner) must approve it with their PIN. A price can only go down, never up.

  1. In the sale, find the line and press Change price.
  2. Type the new price in New price for Bread (an amount such as 12 or 7,50). It must be lower than the current one. Otherwise: The new price must be lower than R15,00. A price can only be lowered.
  3. Press Ask a manager. (Not now cancels.)
  4. You see A manager or the owner must approve a lower price with their PIN. The manager chooses their name in Manager, types their Manager PIN and presses Approve.
  5. The line now shows Price approved by Sipho. The new price is in the total.

To undo it, press Remove price change on that line.

  • The PIN is checked on the till, so approval works offline. The server checks the manager's role again later.
  • A wrong PIN says That PIN is not right. Tries left: 3. Too many wrong tries: Too many wrong tries. Try again in a few minutes.
  • If there is no manager on the till: There is no manager on this till to approve it. The owner can sign in and approve it.
  • A price-labelled item cannot be changed: A labelled item cannot be changed. Remove it and scan again.

For managers: a lower price is lost money. Only type your PIN yourself and only when you have seen why. Never give your PIN to a cashier to "press for you". Your name is stored on the sale line.

10Refunds and cancelling a sale

A refund takes some or all of the goods back and returns the money. A cancellation voids the whole sale. Both are done from the Past sales tab, and both go back to stock.

Find the sale

Open Past sales. It lists the recent sales rung up on this till, with Time, Items, Total and Paid by. Type an item name or an amount in Find by item name or amount, then press Open on the right row. If the list is empty: No sales on this till yet.

Past sales shows what this till sold recently. For an old sale from another till, ask a manager to look it up in the back office.

Take goods back (a refund)

  1. On the sale, under Take goods back, type for each item the quantity coming back (Quantity to take back of Bread (up to 3)). You can never return more than was sold, less what was already returned.
  2. Type the Reason for the refund. A reason is needed: Say why the goods are coming back.
  3. Check Refund: R30,00 and the line under it, The money goes back to the customer the way it was paid: cash. (for a credit sale: The amount comes off what the customer owes.)
  4. Press Refund. Refund of R30,00 saved.

If the shop has not switched refunds on you will see Refunds are not switched on for this shop. If everything has already been taken back: Everything on this sale has been taken back.

Refunds over the limit

A refund above the shop's limit needs a manager. The limit is R500 unless the shop set a different one. For a cashier the button becomes Ask a manager and the screen says A refund above R500,00 needs a manager to approve it. The manager picks their name, types their Manager PIN and presses Approve. A manager refunding above the limit approves it with their own sign-in.

For managers: check the customer and the goods yourself before you approve. The stock goes back on the shelf, and the money leaves the drawer. Cash refunds are taken off what the till expects at cash-up, so an unrecorded cash refund shows up as a shortage.

Sales paid in several ways, by wallet, voucher or lay-by

These cannot be refunded at the till. The screen says This sale was paid by split, wallet, voucher or lay-by, so it is refunded through the server, not at the till. You use Refund online, which needs the internet and a sale that has already reached the server.

  1. Fill in quantities and the reason as above.
  2. Choose Pay the refund back as: Gift voucher (the default), Cash, Card or QR.
  3. Press Refund online (or Ask a manager over the limit).
  4. For a gift voucher the screen shows Gift voucher code: … Give it to the customer now. It is shown only once.

Possible messages: This till is not online. Connect to the internet to refund this sale., This sale has not reached the server yet. Wait for the till to finish sending, then try again., That refund was already made.

Cancel the whole sale (managers only)

A cashier does not see this section. A manager or owner can cancel a sale if it was rung up wrongly.

  1. Open the sale in Past sales.
  2. Under Cancel the whole sale press Cancel this sale.
  3. Type the Reason for cancelling.
  4. Press Confirm cancellation. The sale was cancelled. A cancelled sale shows a Cancelled mark.
  • If part of the sale was already refunded, it cannot be cancelled: Part of this sale was already taken back, so it cannot be cancelled.
  • Cancelling a credit sale also takes the amount off the customer's account.
  • The shop owner is alerted when a till has many cancellations in an hour. It is one of the ways the owner spots theft.

Cancelling does not hand money back for you. If the customer paid cash, you still have to count it back out, and it must be recorded properly. Do not cancel to "fix" a till that is short.

To throw away a sale that has not been paid yet, use Clear sale on the Sell tab instead.

11Tickets, tables and the kitchen

Cafes and restaurants keep a sale open while the customer eats and pay at the end. These tabs appear only if the owner chose a business type with tickets, tables and a kitchen. They need the internet, because several tills share the same open tickets.

Open tickets and tables

The Tables tab has two views: Floor plan and Open tickets. On the floor plan each table shows Free or Open: R85,00. If there are no tables yet: No tables on the floor plan yet. The owner sets them up in the back office. You can still open a ticket without a table. If tickets are not switched on for the shop you see Tables need open tickets switched on for this shop.

  1. Press a free table (or New ticket without a table).
  2. Type the Ticket name or number (for example Table 4 or Takeaway 12, up to 40 characters).
  3. Add the items, then press Open ticket.

Open a ticket from the list with Open. The ticket tells you who has it:

Held by this till
You can change it.
Another till is working on it
You can look but not change it: Another till is working on this ticket, so it is read only.
Free to take
Press Take it to edit first.

When you hold a ticket you can Save changes, Move it to another table, Merge another ticket into it, or Give it back so other tills can work on it. A hold lapses after 10 minutes if nothing happens, so give a ticket back when you are done.

Paying a ticket

Press Take payment (a ticket with no items says A ticket with no items cannot be paid.). Choose Paid by Cash, Card or QR, and for cash type Cash received. Press Take R85,00 and close the ticket. It confirms Paid R85,00. The ticket is closed. and shows the change.

If the sale is saved but the server could not close the ticket yet, the screen says The sale is saved on this till. The server could not close the ticket yet; press the button again. Press it again. It will not charge twice.

Or press Move to the sale on the till to put the ticket's items on the Sell tab, where you can use the normal payment screens (including split payments and vouchers).

Voiding a ticket

Void ticket throws the items away and cannot be undone: Void ticket Table 4? Its items are thrown away and it cannot be undone. Choose Yes, void it only when you are sure, or Keep it.

A voided ticket loses food that may already have been made. Voids are visible to the owner. Never void a ticket to hide a sale.

Sending to the kitchen

The Kitchen tab has two views: Send to the kitchen and Kitchen board.

  1. On Send to the kitchen, an Order number is filled in. Keep it, or type your own (letters, digits and dashes, up to 12 characters). Each number is used once.
  2. Type the Table or name and add the items. Or choose Fill from an open ticket.
  3. Press Send to the kitchen. Order K-1042 sent to the kitchen.

If the number was used already, a new one is filled in. Send again. If no kitchen printer or screen is set up you see No kitchen screen or printer is set up on this till. The order is on the kitchen board. That is normal for now. See Printer, drawer and scale.

12Quotes and lay-by

A quote holds a price for a customer. A lay-by lets a customer pay in parts and collect the goods when it is paid off. Both need the internet.

Quotes

Open the Quotes tab. The list shows Quote, Customer, Total, Valid until and Status (Open, Turned into a sale, Expired, Cancelled).

  1. Press New quote.
  2. Keep the Quote number (it is filled in; up to 20 characters), type the Customer name and Customer phone, and pick Valid until (from today, up to 90 days).
  3. Add the items and press Save quote. Quote Q-0001 saved.

For a saved quote, the row has buttons: Download Q-0001 as PDF, Add Q-0001 to the sale on the till, Convert Q-0001 to a sale (turns it into an open ticket; then Take payment now or Pay later from the open tickets) and Cancel Q-0001 (The customer's price is no longer held.).

When you add a quote to the sale it becomes lines on the Sell tab at the quoted price, and the quote is marked turned into a sale. If it could not be marked, the quote stays open until you are back online. If you then give up the sale with Clear sale, the quote opens again.

Lay-by

Open the Lay-by tab. The shop sets the rules (a minimum deposit and a longest time to pay off). The screen reminds you: The deposit is at least 10% of the total. The lay-by must be paid off within 12 weeks. (the real numbers are the shop's).

  1. Press Start a lay-by.
  2. Fill in the Lay-by reference (pre-filled), Customer name and Customer phone.
  3. Add the items. Type the Deposit (the screen says At least R…) and choose Deposit paid by Cash, Card or QR. Pick Pay off by.
  4. Press Start lay-by. Lay-by L-0001 started. R300,00 still owing.

Later, open the lay-by with Open. You see Payments so far and Still owing R300,00 of R450,00. To take a payment type the Payment, choose Paid by and press Take payment. You cannot take more than is owed.

13Serial numbers, customers and appointments

Serial numbers

Some items, such as a phone or a tool, are tracked by serial number. After a sale that includes one, the sale screen offers: These items have serial numbers. Type them now, or later in the Serial numbers tab. You get a box for each unit: Serial number 1 for Drill.

  1. Type the serial numbers from the box or the item.
  2. Press Save serial numbers. Serial numbers saved. They go to the server once the sale has arrived. Or press Not now and do it later.

If you saved none: Type at least one serial number. A serial number can be sold once only.

Later, in the Serial numbers tab, Record against a sale lets you choose a recent sale (Choose one of this till's recent sales) or type its reference, choose the Item, type the serial number, press Add serial number, then Record serial numbers. Find a serial number searches what was sold (type at least 3 characters).

If the server does not have the sale yet: The server does not have that sale yet. It is still waiting to be sent from this till. Try again in a moment.

Customers and loyalty

The Customers tab finds people by phone number or name (Find a customer by phone number or name; type at least 3 characters). A row shows Name, Phone and (with loyalty) Points.

  1. If nobody is found: No customer found. You can add them below. Press Add a customer.
  2. Type the Name and Cell phone number (a South African number, for example 082 123 4567). Email is optional.
  3. Tick the consent box only if the customer said yes to receiving messages (receipts, specials, reminders).
  4. Press Save customer. Thandi M. was saved.

Do not tick the consent box on your own. A customer who did not agree must not be messaged. The customer's page says Has not agreed to messages. Do not message this customer. if they did not.

Open a customer to see their points (120 points, worth R12,00.), the Recent points list (Earned, Used, Adjusted, Taken back) and the button Add Name to the sale on the till. Points are earned by the sale and are taken back if the sale is refunded or cancelled. To pay with points in a sale, see Vouchers, points and the school wallet.

  1. Press Book an appointment.
  2. Choose the Service and who it is With. Choose the Date and Time (on the hour or in 5-minute steps).

14Stock and transfers

Managers and the owner receive deliveries, count shelves and write off losses at the till. Cashiers do not see these tabs.

The stock list

Open Stock. The Stock list shows each Product with how many are On hand and Notes: Out, Low, Not selling, Expired, Expiring soon (within 14 days) and Needs a count. Offline it says The till is offline, so these are the figures from the last time it was online. Press Refresh when online. On a till that has never loaded the figures: No stock figures yet. Connect the till to the internet once to load them.

Receive stock

  1. Press Receive stock.
  2. Optionally type the Supplier and Invoice number.
  3. Scan or type each product (Scan a barcode or type a product name). For each, type the Quantity (a whole number above zero) and the Cost each (for example 12 or 12,50).
  4. Press Save delivery. Delivery saved: 6 products.

Check the delivery against the supplier's invoice before you save. A wrong cost changes the shop's profit figures.

Count

  1. Press Count and choose the product. The till says The till has 12 of Milk.
  2. Count the shelf. Type the number in Counted, pick a Reason and press Save count.
  3. If it matches: The count matches. Nothing to change. Otherwise: Saved. Milk: -2 (now 10).

If the till does not know how many there should be: Connect to the internet and refresh the stock list first.

Write off

Goods that are expired, damaged, missing or taken for own use must leave stock, so the figures stay honest.

  1. Press Write off and choose the product.
  2. Type How many to write off and the Reason: Expired, Damaged, Missing or Own use.
  3. Press Confirm write-off. Written off: 3 of Yoghurt.

A write-off is a loss the owner sees. Always choose the true reason. A count with a loss reason is recorded as a write-off too. Stock work done offline is saved on the till and sent later, like a sale.

Transfers between branches

If the owner has several branches and the plan allows it, Transfers moves stock between them. It needs the internet: Stock transfers need the internet. Connect the till and try again. Three views:

  • Coming in: stock on its way to this shop. For each transfer, type Received quantity of Milk and press Receive. If less arrived the screen shows Milk: 8 of 10 received (short by 2). Press Cancel transfer if it is not coming.
  • Sent: stock this shop sent, with its status (on its way, received, cancelled).
  • Send stock: type the other branch's number (from the back office), add the products and quantities, and press Send stock. Stock sent. The other branch can now receive it. You can only send to your own branches.

If the plan does not allow it: Your plan does not include moving stock between branches. Ask the owner about upgrading.

15Cash-up at the end of the day

Count the drawer, compare it with what the till expects, save. It takes five minutes and it is how the shop catches shortages early.

  1. Finish selling. Make sure any credit payments and refunds of the day are done.
  2. Open the Cash-up tab. It says Count the cash in the drawer and compare it with what the till expects for 2026-11-11.
  3. Check the Float (cash the drawer started the day with). It is filled with the last float you used. Correct it if today's was different.
  4. Read the table: Float, Cash sales, Paid on credit accounts in cash, Cash given back in refunds and Expected in the drawer.
  5. Now count the drawer, and type it in Cash counted in the drawer. Count first, type second.
  6. The till tells you: Short by R20,00, Over by R5,00 or The drawer balances. Add a Note (optional) to explain.
  7. Press Save cash-up. Cash-up saved for 2026-11-11. The drawer balances.
  • The tips are in the drawer too, but they are not sales. The owner will tell you how the shop counts them.
  • Only the cash part of a split payment counts as cash.
  • A second cash-up for the same day is refused by the server: A cash-up for … was already saved from this till. The server keeps one per day, so a second one may be refused.
  • Cash-up works offline. The server works out the expected cash again from the sales that really arrived.

Do not change the counted amount to match what is expected. A shortage that is written down honestly can be understood. A hidden one cannot. The owner is alerted when cash-ups are short.

16Working offline

The internet will drop. The till is built so that you can keep selling. Know what still works, and what has to wait.

Saved on the till first

When you finish a sale, the till writes it into a queue on the till itself (the outbox) before it says Sale saved. Nothing waits for the network. If the internet is down, or the till is switched off right after, the sale is still there. When a connection returns, the till sends the queue by itself. If sending fails it tries again, waiting longer each time (from 5 seconds up to 5 minutes). A sale only leaves the till after the server has confirmed it has it.

What works offline

  • Selling: scan, search, weighed items, promotions, cash, card, QR, credit, split payments of cash, card and QR.
  • Unlocking with your PIN, and manager approval of a lower price or a refund.
  • Refunds and cancellations of sales paid by cash, card, QR or credit (saved on the till and sent later).
  • Cash-up, the credit book (with the last copy of the balances), the stock list (last copy), receiving, counting and writing off stock.
  • Serial numbers typed straight after a sale (they wait until the sale has arrived).

  • Online refunds: sales paid by split, wallet, voucher or lay-by.
  • The tabs Tables, Kitchen, Quotes, Lay-by, Vouchers, Customers, Appointments, Serial numbers, School wallet and Transfers (they are greyed out).
  • Finding a customer by phone number, and getting a QR slip.
  • Downloading new prices, staff or the owner's changes, and the owner's approval of a new till.

If a customer wants to pay with a voucher, points or a school wallet and the internet is down, ask them to pay another way, or to come back. Do not accept a voucher code and promise to "sort it out later": the till cannot check the balance, and the shop may never be paid.

If you take something from a voucher, points or a wallet and then give up the sale (Back, Clear sale), the till gives it back for you: The voucher amount was given back., The points were given back. or The wallet payment was cancelled and the money returned. If the till is offline at that moment: Something taken from a voucher, points or wallet will be given back as soon as the till is online. It does this by itself, even if the till is closed and opened again.

17Expired items

Selling food past its expiry date is dangerous and against the law. The system is designed to stop it.

The rule is simple. An item whose expiry date has passed blocks the sale unless a manager approves it with their PIN. An item that expires today can still be sold. When a manager approves, their name is kept on the sale line, and the server checks that their role is allowed to approve.

Honest status. The expiry rule and its checks are built into the till's rules and the server, but the Sell screen in this version does not yet show a special expired-item message or a separate override button. The only expiry warnings you will see on the till today are the Expired and Expiring soon notes in the Stock list (managers). Do not rely on the till to stop you. Check the date on the pack, and tell the owner if the till sold something that was off.

What to do in practice:

  1. Look at the date on anything perishable you sell.
  2. If it is past the date, do not sell it. Set it aside for the manager.
  3. A manager writes it off in Stock → Write off with the reason Expired.
  4. Only a manager may decide to sell an expired item at all. It is a decision that the shop and the manager answer for.

18Printer, drawer and scale

An honest word first: the Till app is written to work with a receipt printer, a cash drawer, a weighing scale and a kitchen printer or screen, but the real device connections are not written yet.

The project's build status says the rules and slip layouts exist and are tested against pretend devices. The part that talks to real hardware (over USB, Bluetooth or Web Serial) is not written, because it needs the printer and scale models to be chosen and a test on real equipment. So today, on a real till:

  • No slip prints by itself, and Print slip with QR does not appear.
  • The cash drawer does not open by itself. Open it with the key.
  • The Read from scale button does not appear. Type the weight in grams yourself.
  • Kitchen orders go to the kitchen board on screen, not to a kitchen printer.

When the shop's hardware is connected in a later version, the till will do these things for you, and it will describe problems in plain words:

21Glossary

Add-on
An extra item (a side, a bag) that is a product with its own price and stock.
Approve
When a manager or owner types their PIN to allow a lower price or a large refund.
Back office
The owner's website for staff, tills, products and reports. The till does not do these things.
Cash-up
The end-of-day count of the cash drawer against what the till expects.
Credit (customer)
A regular customer who buys now and pays later, up to a limit.
Float
The cash the drawer starts the day with.
Held ticket
A sale kept open (for a table or a customer) to be paid later.
Lay-by
Goods set aside while the customer pays them off in parts.
Lock (PIN)
The 5-minute pause after five wrong PIN tries.
Offline
The till has no connection. It can still sell. Sales are sent later.
Outbox
The queue on the till where every sale and record waits until the server confirms it.
Override (price)
A manager-approved lower price on one sale line.
Past sales
The tab where you find recent sales to refund or cancel.
Promotion
An automatic discount, such as a percentage off, a fixed price or a combo.
QR slip
A free digital slip: a web address the customer opens on their phone.
Refused (sale)
A sale the server would not accept. It is kept and shown in the top bar so the owner can look at it.
Setup code
The one-time code the owner gets when a till is added. It proves the till belongs to the shop.
Split payment
One sale paid in more than one way, up to six, each way once.
Sync
The till sending its queue to the server, and receiving staff, products and promotions.
Tender
The way a customer pays: cash, card, QR, credit, voucher, points or wallet.
Voucher
A gift or refund code worth a set amount of money. Its code is shown only once.
Wallet (school)
A learner's or teacher's prepaid account at a school tuckshop, read from a phone QR code or a card or wristband.
Write-off
Taking goods out of stock because they are expired, damaged, missing or used by the owner.
