1Welcome

TillKnowIT puts your products in front of spaza shops that already use the TillKnowIT till. They see your prices, order from you, and you get paid through the platform.

This manual covers two web apps that suppliers use. Both sign in the same way and both work on a phone or a computer.

The two apps
AppWhat it is forMenu entries
Supplier portalYour day-to-day work: applying, price list, orders, payment statements, deals, plan and campaigns.Application, Catalogue, Orders, Deals, Plan and campaigns
Insights portalWeekly sales trends for an area, grouped so no shop can be identified. Needs a paid plan.Market trends, Plan

How a supplier works with the platform

  1. You apply. Your application stays pending until the platform checks and verifies you.
  2. You load your price list and set the areas and days you deliver to.
  3. Shops in those areas order from you. You reply line by line.
  4. You deliver. The shop confirms what arrived and the money is released to you.

What is real and what is not yet. The software for payments, subscriptions and plans is built and tested. But no real payment partner is connected yet: only a test (development) payment fake exists, so no real money moves today. The same is true of WhatsApp and email delivery of orders and of the card gateway for subscriptions. The screens in this manual are also tested against fake data only. Where this matters, we say so in the chapter.

2Signing in

You sign in with your cell phone number and a code. There is no password to remember.

  1. Open the portal. On the sign-in screen, type your cell phone number under Your cell phone number, for example 082 123 4567.
  2. Press Send me a code.
  3. Type the code under The code we sent you and press Sign in.
  4. If you work for more than one shop or supplier, choose one under Who are you working for?.

To use another number, press Use a different number. To leave, press Sign out. If you are new, choose I am new here: create an account and type Your name first.

Demo sign-in. To try the portals with sample data, sign in as the demo supplier Sello Mahlangu with phone 082 555 0006. This works in both portals. In demo mode the code is shown on screen, under the box: The server sent the code back so you can test without a phone. This never happens in the real service, where the code arrives as a message.

If you see Too many tries. Please wait a few minutes and try again., wait and try again. If your sign-in runs out you will see Your sign-in has run out. Please sign in again.

Once you are signed in as someone who works for a supplier, the portal finds your supplier for you and shows Working as followed by your business name at the top of each screen. If it cannot find one, it asks Which supplier? and you can type a supplier id once. A new supplier does not need this: applying fills it in.

3Applying as a supplier

Before you can sell, the platform checks your business. You apply once, and you can watch the status on the same screen.

Open Application in the menu. Under Apply to become a supplier fill in the following.

The application form
FieldWhat to type
Business nameYour trading name. Required.
Registration number (optional)Your company registration number, if you have one. The platform checks that it has the right shape.
Contact personThe person shops and the platform should speak to.
Phone (optional)Your office or sales phone.
WhatsApp (optional)The number for WhatsApp orders.
Email (optional)The address for email orders and notices.
Municipality codeThe area you deliver to, for example JHB.
Delivery daysThe days you deliver in that area.

To add another area, press Add another area. To drop one, use Remove area with its number. Then press Send application. You need at least one area with a delivery day, or you will see Add at least one area with a delivery day.

When it works you see Application sent. with your status and your supplier id. The id is saved in this browser.

Keep your supplier id safe. If you sign in on a different phone or computer, the portal normally finds your supplier from your organisation, but a copy of the id is a good backup.

Reading your status

Under Your supplier the screen shows the following, once you have a supplier.

  • Verified: Not yet while you are pending, Yes once the platform has verified you.
  • Minimum order, Delivery fee and Payment terms.
  • Areas and delivery days, or No delivery areas yet.
  • If the platform declined you: Why it was not accepted followed by the reason. Fix the problem and speak to the platform before applying again.

The platform works through a four-item checklist and all four items are required. A rejection always needs a written reason, so you will always be told why. Once you are verified, your organisation becomes a verified one.

If you already have a supplier id from the platform, type it into Supplier id and press Show my supplier.

4Inviting your reps

A rep is a colleague who can work on your orders. They get access through their own phone number.

  1. Open Catalogue and choose the Reps tab.
  2. Under Invite a rep, type Rep name and Rep phone.
  3. Press Invite rep. You will see {name} was invited. with their name.

The rep then signs in with that phone number and the code sent to it, and works under your supplier. Only someone who is already allowed to act for your supplier can invite reps. Reps receive the supplier rep role.

Type the rep's phone number carefully. Access is tied to the number, so a wrong digit may give access to the wrong person.

5Your catalogue and price list

Your catalogue is your list of products with case sizes and case prices. The quickest way to build it is to import your own spreadsheet.

Open Catalogue. It has five tabs: Products, Import, Areas and days, Terms and Reps.

Importing a price list

Import accepts a spreadsheet or CSV file: .csv, .xlsx or .xls. It is a two-step process on purpose, so you can check before anything is saved.

  1. Open the Import tab. Under Import a price list, choose your file in Price list file.
  2. Press Preview. If you forgot the file you will see Choose a file first.
  3. Read the summary: Preview: {created} new, {updated} changed, {unchanged} unchanged, {errors} with errors. Nothing is saved yet.
  4. Look at the table with the columns Row, Product, Action and Notes. Rows with errors explain the problem in Notes.
  5. If you are happy, press Save the import. You will see Saved: with the final counts.

If you choose a different file after a preview, the preview is cleared and you must preview again. The button Save the import only works after a preview.

How columns are matched

You do not need to rename your columns. The platform looks at the headings in your file and matches them to the fields it needs: the product barcode, the product name, how many units are in a case, the case price, the unit price and whether the price includes VAT. The headings it recognised are reported back to you, under the title Columns we found.

What each row should tell us
InformationWhy it matters
BarcodeThe platform matches your product to the shared catalogue by barcode, so a shop sees the same product with the same name whichever supplier sells it.
NameUsed when no barcode match exists, and shown to shops.
Units per caseHow many items are in one case. Shops order in cases.
Case priceYour price for one case, in rand.
Unit priceThe price of a single item, worked out per unit.
VAT flagWhether your price already includes VAT. It affects the true cost a shop sees when comparing suppliers.

Always use the barcode printed on the product. Products matched by barcode are the ones shops find fastest, and it avoids duplicate entries of the same item.

Check the case size and the VAT setting in the preview. A case price typed for a different case size, or a price marked as VAT-inclusive when it is not, changes every order at that price.

You can repeat an import whenever your prices change. Existing products are counted as changed or unchanged, not duplicated.

The product list and editing one product

The Products tab, headed Your products, shows Product, Barcode, Case, Case price, Unit price and Stock (In stock or Out of stock). With no products it says No products yet. Import a price list to add some.

Press Edit beside a product to change it. You can change Name, Units per case, Case price (rand), Price includes VAT, In stock and Expiry policy (optional), then Save. Use Cancel to leave without changing.

Prices are typed in rand, for example 148.50. Units per case must be a whole number above zero. Mark an item In stock off when you run out, so shops do not order it.

6Delivery areas, days and terms

Shops only find you in the areas you serve, and they see your minimum order and fee before they order.

Areas and days

On the Catalogue screen open Areas and days. Under Delivery areas and days each area has a Municipality code and Delivery days. Use Add another area and Remove area, then Save. If you have none, it says No delivery areas yet. Add one so shops can find you.

Areas come from the platform's list of municipalities. The eight metros are already on it, and the platform's administrators add more on request.

Order terms

Open the Terms tab, Order terms.

The terms
FieldMeaning
Minimum order (rand)The smallest order you accept. An order below it is flagged to you as This order is below your minimum.
Delivery fee (rand)Added to every order.
Payment termsChoose Cash on delivery, Pay in the app or Both.

Type amounts in rand, such as 500 or 25.50. Press Save; you will see Saved.

The minimum order and delivery fee apply to every order and to every group deal order. Set them before you promote your products.

7Orders from shops

Shops can send you an order through the app, by WhatsApp or by email. Whichever way they send it, you answer it in the portal.

Open Orders. The first tab is Incoming orders. Use Show to filter by All orders, Waiting for your reply, Confirmed, Delivered, Disputed or Cancelled. The list shows Order, Status, Payment, Delivery date and Total. Press Open on a row.

Orders sent by WhatsApp or email reach you through those channels, but a real WhatsApp and email provider is not yet connected. Today the app route is the one fully proven end to end, using test fakes.

Reading an order

The order opens under Order {ref} with a line for each product: Ordered, Confirmed, Delivered, Case price and the line's status. The total shows delivery fee and VAT included.

Replying to an order

An order that is Waiting for your reply shows Reply to this order. For each line choose what you will do.

  • Confirm as ordered: you will supply what was asked, at the listed price.
  • Change: type different cases and, if you like, a new case price (rand, optional).
  • Decline this line: you cannot supply it.

Choose a Delivery date and press Send reply. You will see Reply sent for {ref}. The shop is told what you decided for each line. Cases must be whole numbers and prices in rand like 148.50, or you will see the message Cases must be a whole number, and prices in rand like 148.50.

Replying is per line, so a half-confirmed order is normal. Check each line. A line you confirm is a promise.

Delivering

Once confirmed, the order shows Mark as delivered. Each line lists cases delivered, already filled with the confirmed number. Change a number only if you delivered fewer cases than confirmed. Press Mark delivered. You will see {ref} was marked delivered.

The shop then confirms what it received. If the shop's count is lower than yours, the shop's count is the one that wins, and the money for the missing cases is returned (see the next chapter).

Cash orders

For a delivered order still owing, you will see Mark as paid. Press it when the shop has paid you in cash on delivery. You will see {ref} was marked paid.

Press Mark as paid only when you really have the cash in your hand. It records that the shop has settled the order.

8Getting paid

A shop can pay in the app or pay you in cash on delivery. In both cases the platform keeps a careful record, so disputes can be settled fairly.

The money rules
SituationWhat happens
Shop pays in the appThe money is held by the platform until the order is delivered. It is not yet yours.
Delivery confirmed by the shopThe held money is released to you.
Shop does not confirmThe money is released to you automatically after 72 hours.
Short deliveryThe shop's count wins. The difference is refunded to the shop and you receive the rest.
DisputeThe money is frozen until a platform administrator resolves it.
Cash on deliveryYou collect the cash and press Mark as paid.

Honest status. The payment rules above are built and tested, including signed payment messages, a replay guard and a daily reconciliation to the cent. But no real payment partner is connected yet. Only a development fake exists, so no real money moves until a partner agreement is in place. Treat this chapter as describing how it will work.

Raising a dispute

If something went wrong with a delivered order, open it and use Raise a dispute. Describe the problem under What went wrong? and press Raise dispute. You will see: The dispute was raised. A platform admin will look at it. The money is held until then.

Only platform administrators can list disputes, so they do not appear as a list in this portal. After raising one, the order shows as Disputed in your orders list.

Your statement

Open Orders and choose the Statement tab. It lists your orders by the day they were sent. Choose From and To dates, or leave them empty for everything. Choose a Format: CSV, Excel (xlsx) or PDF. Press Download statement. The file is saved and you will see Statement {name} was downloaded.

Send your bookkeeper the Excel or CSV version at month end. It is the simplest way to match platform orders to your own books.

9Deals

A deal is a time-limited offer on your own products, shown to shops in the areas you choose.

Open Deals. The Deals tab lists Your deals with Deal, Type, Runs, Areas and State (Running or Not running). Press Cancel to stop one; you will see {title} was cancelled.

Posting a deal

Use Post a deal. Enter a Title and choose a Type.

Deal types
TypeWhat you fill in
Discount on a productChoose the Product. Then give Percent off (1 to 90) or Or rand off per case. Give a percentage or an amount, not both.
Free goods (buy some, get some free)The product, Cases to buy and Cases free.
Combo of productsTwo or more products, each with Cases of product, using Add a product to the combo, and one Price for the whole combo (rand).

Then set Starts and Ends, and Areas (municipality codes, separated by commas). You can only name areas you deliver to. Press Post deal. If you have no products you will see Import products first: a deal needs one of your products.

A deal reduces your income on every case sold. Check the percentage, the end date and the areas before you post. Amounts are typed in rand, for example 12.50.

Sponsored deals

Tick Make this a sponsored deal to have the deal pushed to shops. A sponsored deal needs a running sponsored campaign on the Premium plan (chapter 12). Each showing is billed by use and is always labelled Sponsored to shops. You will see the label Sponsored in your deals list too.

Sponsoring does not hide you from the comparison. With default settings a sponsored offer never ranks above a cheaper offer that is not sponsored. If a campaign budget is used up, the deal continues to be shown, but without the label and without any boost.

10Group deals

A group deal lets many shops buy together to reach a bulk price. You post the price and a minimum; the shops do the rest.

Open Deals and choose the Group deals tab. You see Your group deals. Shops join together; you see how many cases and shops, never which shops.

Under Post a group deal fill in:

  • Product and Bulk price per case (rand).
  • Minimum total cases: the number of cases that must be reached.
  • Closes: the closing date.
  • Area code: the area whose shops can join.
  • Buying group id (optional): fill this in to make the deal Invite only for the members of one buying group.

Press Post group deal. The table shows Product, Bulk price, Progress (for example 40 of 100 cases (40%), 6 shops), Closes and Status.

What happens at the deadline

  • If the minimum was reached, each shop gets one order from you at the bulk price. Your normal terms and delivery fee apply.
  • If it was not reached, everyone is told and nobody is charged.

Once the minimum is met you can press Close early. You can also use Cancel to cancel the deal.

Once the minimum is reached, you will have to supply every order at the bulk price. Only set a minimum and a price you can honour.

Buying groups (stokvels, schools and similar) and invite codes work in the platform, but there is no screen in the portal to manage a group. A group's admin shares an invite code with members; you only type the buying group id when posting.

Every supplier starts on the free plan. A paid plan adds features such as sponsored campaigns and market reports.

Open Plan and campaigns and choose the Plan and invoices tab.

Choosing a plan

Under Supplier plans the table shows Plan, Price per month and Includes. The features it can list are Sponsored campaigns, Supplier analytics, Market insight reports and Partner API. The free plan is marked Free and includes The basics. Your current plan shows Your plan. Press Choose on another plan.

Invoices

Under Invoices you see Invoice, Status, Due and Total (VAT included). Press Pay to pay an open invoice (you will see Invoice {number} was paid.), or the lines button to see Lines of {number}. A copy of each invoice, with VAT, is also emailed to you.

If a payment fails, the platform retries and sends reminders on set days. Extras are limited after a grace period.

12Sponsored campaigns

A sponsored campaign shows your deal to shops in areas you choose. You pay only for showings, and never more than your budget.

Open Plan and campaigns and choose Sponsored campaigns. Campaigns are part of the Premium plan. Without it you will see Sponsored campaigns need the Premium plan. Choose Premium on the Plan and invoices tab to switch them on.

Starting a campaign

  1. Under Start a campaign, type a Campaign name.
  2. Optionally type the Deal id to promote (optional).
  3. Set Budget (rand) and Price per showing (rand), for example 500 and 0.50.
  4. List Areas (municipality codes, separated by commas).
  5. Choose Starts on and Ends on.
  6. Press Start campaign.

Managing campaigns

Your campaigns shows Campaign, Budget, Spent, Areas and Runs. Use Pause and Resume. Usage shows a line such as 120 showings so far, R60.00 spent, R440.00 left.

How billing works

  • You are billed by use. Each showing costs the price per showing.
  • A showing of an offer to the same shop, for the same deal, is charged once per day.
  • The campaign stops when its budget is used up. You are never charged above the budget.
  • It is always labelled Sponsored to shops.

A campaign spends real money from your budget each time it is shown. Start with a small budget and watch Usage for the first few days. Use Pause if the result is not what you wanted.

13Ratings and demand totals

Two screens under Orders show how shops see you and what they are running short of.

Ratings

After a delivery, a shop can rate you: was it on time, was it complete, and a score from 1 to 5. Only one rating is allowed per order. The Ratings tab shows Your ratings: {count} rated deliveries, then Average score, On time and Complete percentages, and recent comments. The shop's name is never shown.

Your average only counts in how shops rank offers once you have 3 ratings. Until then you will see Averages show once enough deliveries are rated. Note that ratings are not yet used in the ranking shops see: that part is still to be built.

The best rating tip is simple: deliver the cases you confirmed, on the date you gave. Change a line before you reply, not after.

Demand

The Demand tab shows shops that opted in to sharing and that are running low on your products, by area. For each area and product you see Shops running low, a count. These are counts only: no shop names, no sales and no stock levels. An area or product with fewer than 3 shops is left out, so a figure can never point at one shop. If nothing qualifies, you will see Nothing to show yet.

A shop that has not chosen to share is not counted at all. Use this screen to decide where to promote a deal or which product to keep in stock.

14The Insights portal

The Insights portal shows weekly sales trends for an area. It is built to be useful to you without ever showing you, or anyone, a single shop's figures.

Sign in the same way as the Supplier portal (demo: Sello Mahlangu, 082 555 0006). The menu has Market trends and Plan.

Asking for a trend report

  1. Open Market trends. Under Weekly trends for an area, type the Municipality code, for example JHB.
  2. Optionally type a Category (optional) to narrow the report.
  3. Choose From and To. They start filled in with the last eight weeks.
  4. Press Show trends.

The table, Trends for {area}, has Week starting, Category, Units sold, Revenue band and Shops behind it. If there is nothing, it says No weeks to show for this area and period. If you forget a field you will see Type the municipality code of the area. or Choose the first and last day.

The 10-shop minimum

The box Why some areas show nothing explains the rule. A figure is only published when at least 10 shops are behind it, and only shops that chose to share. Revenue is a band, not an exact amount, and no shop is named. Shops behind it is the lowest number of shops in that week and is always 10 or more. If an area or category has fewer than 10 shops, its weeks are left out. Try a bigger area, a wider period or no category.

This rule is enforced twice: in the software, and by a check in the database itself, so rows with fewer than 10 shops are never even stored.

What the data is, and is not

Understanding the figures
It isIt is not
Weekly totals of units sold and a revenue band, for an area and category.A list of shops, or any one shop's sales.
Built only from shops that opted in and are verified.A census of every shop in the area. Shops that did not opt in are absent.
A guide to where demand is rising or falling.Your own sales, stock or customer data.
Grouped, with no shop, person or device on any row.A way to find out who bought what. Do not try to work this out.

Use trends to decide, not to prove. An area with a small number of shops can swing from week to week. Look at several weeks before you act on a change.

Honest status. The insights run inside the main platform for now, not as a separate service with its own data warehouse. The platform's legal and data-ethics review is still to be completed before general release.

The plan

Trend reports are part of the Plus and Premium supplier plans, not the free plan. Without one you will see Market insights are not in your plan. Open Plan. It shows Your subscription and a Plans table with Plan, Price per month and Includes. The feature here is called Market trend reports. Press Choose on the plan you want. You will see You are now on {plan}. Upgrades, downgrades and cancelling work as described in chapter 11.

15Glossary

Barcode
The number on a product's packaging. It is how your product is matched to the shared catalogue.
Bulk price
The lower case price of a group deal.
Case
The unit shops order in: a set number of items, for example 24 cans.
Case price
Your price for one case, in rand.
Dispute
A complaint about a delivered order. The money is frozen until a platform administrator decides.
Group deal
A deal that only goes ahead if enough shops together reach a minimum number of cases.
Held payment
Money paid in the app that waits with the platform until delivery is confirmed, or for 72 hours.
Municipality code
A short code for an area, such as JHB.
Pending
Your application has been sent but the platform has not verified you yet.
Rep
A colleague you invite to work on your orders.
Revenue band
A range, not an exact amount, used in trend reports.
Sponsored
A paid-for showing of a deal. Always labelled to shops.
Statement
A file of your orders for a period, as CSV, Excel or PDF.
Supplier id
The long code that identifies your supplier on the platform.
Verified
The platform has checked your business and accepted it.
