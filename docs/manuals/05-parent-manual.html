1Welcome

Your child goes to a school with a tuck shop that uses TillKnowIT. Instead of carrying cash, your child has a small wallet of money that you fill, and that the tuck shop till can take from. This app is how you look after it.

You do not need to be good with phones to use this app. There are only six places to go, and each one does one job. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can do everything here.

The six places are listed in the menu of the app:

The menu of the Parent app
Menu nameWhat it is for
My childrenSee your children, their balance and daily limit. Link a new child with the code from the school.
Add moneyPut money into a wallet. Move money between your own children.
Spending rulesA daily limit, alerts, and things your child may not buy.
StatementEverything that was bought and added. A QR code to pay with your phone. Block a lost card.
Pre-ordersOrder and pay for a break in advance.
RequestsAsk for your money back when your child leaves, or ask for your child's data to be removed.

If you have more than one child at the school, the screens ask Which child? at the top. Pick the child first, then do what you came to do. Each child has their own wallet.

What you need before you start

  • Your own cell phone number. The app sends you a code by SMS to prove it is you.
  • The 8 letter link code that the school gave you for your child. The school prints it when your child is registered.
  • A bank card or online banking, if you want to add money.

2Your privacy

You are trusted with information about a child, so this app is careful. Here is what it does and does not do, in plain words.

  • You see only your own child. A school wallet shows a parent only their own learner's spending. You cannot see other children, other parents, or what the school sells to anyone else.
  • Nothing is shown until you agree. When you link a child you must tick a box that says you agree that TillKnowIT may keep your child's name, photo, purchases and balance so the school wallet can work. Until you tick it and link the child, you see nothing about them.
  • Up to four parents per child. A child can be linked to at most four parents or guardians, for example both parents and two grandparents. Each one must link with the code and give their own consent.
  • Photos stay at the school. The school may keep a photo so the cashier can check it is your child. Photos are kept at the school tills only; they do not appear in this app.
  • You can ask for everything to be removed. See Requests. The record of the money stays, as the law says, but without any name on it.

The school, not the app, controls your child's name, grade, class, photo, cards and wristbands, and whether a child has left. If any of these are wrong, ask the school.

3Signing in

There is no password to remember. You type your cell number and the app sends you a code.

  1. Open the Parent app. You see the Sign in box.
  2. Type your cell phone number under Your cell phone number. The hint shows the style: 082 123 4567.
  3. Tap Send me a code. The app says: If that number has an account, a code is on its way.
  4. When the SMS arrives, type the code under The code we sent you and tap Sign in.

If you typed the wrong number, tap Use a different number. You will be asked to sign in again after your sign-in runs out (about 12 hours); the app says Your sign-in has run out. Please sign in again. To leave, use Sign out.

If you are a new person, the app offers I am new here: create an account. Type your name as people know you and your number, and follow the same steps.

Trying it out (demo mode)

In a demo or test version of the app, a box labelled Development only appears under the code box. It shows the code on the screen so you can practise without a phone. In the demo, the sample parent is Lerato Khumalo, cell number 082 555 0007. This never happens in the real service: in real life the code comes only by SMS.

SMS sending is built to use a provider called BulkSMS, but it has not been run against the live service yet. Until it is switched on, real codes will not reach a phone.

Never tell anyone your sign-in code, not even someone who says they are from the school or from TillKnowIT. The code gives access to your children's wallets.

4My children: linking a child

Open My children. This is the first place you go, and the place that shows balances at a glance.

What you see

For each child you see their name, the school, and the grade and class (Grade 5, class B), then Balance in big letters. Under it you see the Daily limit, or No daily limit. If the wallet has been stopped you see This wallet is not active. If you have no children linked yet, the app says: No child is linked to you yet. Add one below.

Linking a child

  1. Find the card called Add a child.
  2. Type the 8 letter code the school gave you in Link code. Capital letters are fine.
  3. Read the sentence beside the tick box: I agree that TillKnowIT may keep my child's name, photo, purchases and balance so the school wallet can work. If you agree, tick it.
  4. Tap Add child. The button stays grey until the code is typed and the box is ticked.
  5. You see a green message: (Name) was added. The child now appears in the list.

The link code is private to your family. The school gives each child one code, shown once. Do not post it in a class group. Anyone with the code who also gives consent could link to your child, and a child can have up to four parents.

If the app refuses the code, check each letter with the school's paper. Ask the school for the code again if you have lost it. See When something goes wrong.

There is also a card called Changes the school makes. It reminds you that your child's name, grade, class and photo, new cards and wristbands, and marking a child as having left are all done by the school.

5Add money

Money goes into the wallet only after the payment company confirms that you paid. That keeps your money safe.

Adding money

  1. Open Add money. If you have several children, choose the child under Which child?. You see Balance now.
  2. Type the amount in Amount in rand. You may write 50 or 50,00. The hint says From R20 to R5000. If the amount does not look right the app says Type an amount like 50 or 50,00.
  3. Tap Add money.
  4. A card called Added in this visit appears, with the status Waiting for payment. Tap Go to the payment page and pay by card or by instant EFT.
  5. When you come back, tap Check my balance. When the money arrives the status changes to Money arrived and the balance goes up. If something went wrong it says Payment failed.

The app explains why you must wait: The money is added only after the payment partner confirms your payment. This can take a short while. You do not need to press Add money again; doing so would start a second payment.

Test mode (demo)

In a demo version the payment partner is pretend. The app shows a warning: Test mode: the payment partner is a pretend one. Its payment page does not open and no real money moves. That is normal there. Practise freely.

The real payment partner and the real stored-value wallet partner are not connected yet. Only a pretend partner exists today. Do not expect real money to move until the school or TillKnowIT tells you the real service is switched on.

Cash at the school office

Some schools also accept cash at the school office. The school office records it for you, and it then appears in your Statement as Cash top-up. You do not do this in the app.

Moving money between your own children

If one child has too much and another too little, use the card Move money between my children.

  1. Under Which child? choose the child the money comes from. The card says From (name).
  2. Under Move to choose the other child.
  3. Type the amount in Amount to move in rand and tap Move money.
  4. The app confirms: R20,00 was moved from (name) to (name).

You can only move money between your own children, and you need two children linked. Moving money is safe to repeat if the screen froze: the same request is never done twice.

6Spending rules

You decide how much your child may spend in a day, and what they may not buy. The till follows your rules.

Daily limit and alerts

Open Spending rules. Under Daily limit the app explains: the most your child can spend at the tuck shop in one day. When it is reached, the till says no until tomorrow. You also see Spent today.

  1. Type an amount in Daily limit in rand, for example 20 or 20,50. Leave it empty for no limit.
  2. Tick Tell me when (name) buys something if you want to be told about each purchase.
  3. In Warn me when the balance is below (rand) type an amount to be warned before the money runs out. Leave empty for no warning.
  4. Tap Save. You see Your rules were saved.

The school can also set a ceiling for every wallet. Where both the school's ceiling and your limit apply, the lower one wins.

Things your child may not buy

Under Things (name) may not buy you see what is already blocked, or Nothing is blocked. The tuck shop till refuses blocked items even when there is money.

You can block two kinds of thing:

  • Whole group: nothing in the group can be bought, for example sweets.
  • One item: only that item is refused.
  1. In the card Block something choose what to block under What do you want to block?
  2. Type the Name or code, or tap a suggestion under From the school menu.
  3. Tap Block it. You see Rules saved.

To undo a block, tap Allow again next to it.

A change reaches the till the next time it syncs. It may not take effect on the very next purchase, especially if the till has no data for a while.

7Statement, QR code and cards

The Statement is your proof of every cent: what was bought, what was added, and what the balance was afterwards.

Reading the statement

Under Show choose Last 7 days, Last 30 days or Last 90 days. Under What was bought and added each line has a kind, and below it Balance after. The kinds are:

Kinds of lines on the Statement
KindWhat it means
Top-upYou added money by card or EFT.
Cash top-upCash was paid at the school office.
PurchaseYour child bought something.
Order refundedA pre-order was cancelled and the money returned.
TransferMoney moved between your children.
RefundMoney paid back to you.

If there is nothing, the app says Nothing happened on this wallet in this time. To keep a copy, tap Download spreadsheet (CSV) or Download PDF.

Pay with my phone (QR code)

If your child has a phone, or you are at the tuck shop yourself, you can pay with a QR code instead of a card. Tap Show QR code and show it to the cashier. The code works for about a minute, then it stops. The screen says Works until a time; when it runs out it says This code has expired. Get a new one. Tap New QR code.

A lost card or wristband

The card Cards and wristbands lists your child's cards, each marked Working or Blocked. A card may be a tap card, a QR code or a wristband. Anyone who finds a card could spend what is in the wallet, so act quickly.

  1. To block one, tap Block this card next to it. You see The card was blocked.
  2. To block everything, tap Block all cards, then confirm Yes, block them (or No, keep them to back out). The app says how many were blocked, or There were no active cards to block.

Blocking cannot be undone from the app: Block every card of (name)? This cannot be undone. Blocked cards stop working at every till. Your phone's QR code keeps working. A replacement card must come from the school, and it is linked to the same wallet.

The block reaches tills in the next sync, so a till that has been offline may take a little while to learn about it.

8Pre-orders

Order your child's break in advance and pay now. The money is taken from the wallet when you place the order.

  1. Open Pre-orders. Under Order for a break pick the Break. Each break shows its cut-off, for example Break 1 (order before 09:30).
  2. Pick the Day. It must be a school day, from today up to a week ahead.
  3. Under Menu type how many of each item. The screen shows Total as you go.
  4. Tap Place order. You see Order placed. Total R...

If you choose nothing the app says Choose at least one item. If the school has not set up breaks or a menu, the app says The school has no menu or breaks yet. Prices come from the school's menu.

Your orders

Under My orders you see orders from the last week or coming up, each with a status: Placed, Ready, Collected or Cancelled, and Closes (time).

To cancel, tap Cancel order before the cut-off time. The app says: The order was cancelled and the money is back in the wallet. You get everything back, including any fee, if you cancel before the cut-off.

After the cut-off time an order cannot be cancelled by you. Times are South African time. If you ordered by mistake, be quick.

9Requests

Two serious requests live here: getting your money back when your child leaves, and removing your child's data. Both ask for care.

Get my money back

When a child has left the school, you can ask for the money left in their wallet. Only the school can mark a child as having left. If the school has not done this, ask them first: the screen says Only the school can mark your child as having left.

  1. Open Requests and choose the child.
  2. Under Get my money back tap Ask for a refund.
  3. The app shows the amount and a status. The statuses are waiting for the school, approved, paid and not approved.

The school checks the request, and the money is then paid back to you.

Remove my child's data

You can ask us to delete your child's name, photo and links to you. The record of the money stays, because the law says so, but without any name. All cards are blocked. If there is money left in the wallet it is paid back to you first, and then the data is removed. This takes at most 30 days.

  1. Tap Remove my child's data.
  2. Read the question: Remove all of (name)'s data? This cannot be undone.
  3. Tap Yes, remove it, or No, keep it to stop.
  4. If the wallet is empty the data is removed at once. Otherwise you see Your request was received. It will be done by (date). Money left in the wallet must be refunded first.

Removing a child's data cannot be undone. Your child will no longer have a wallet that you can see. Do this only when you are sure, for example after the child has left and the refund is done.

10What is not ready yet

We prefer to tell you honestly what is not finished.

  • Real payments. Card and EFT top-ups are built to go through a licensed payment partner and to be credited only when the partner's signed message arrives. Only a pretend partner is connected so far, so no real money moves yet.
  • Real SMS codes. The sign-in code is built for the BulkSMS provider, which has not run against the live service yet.
  • Refunds and payouts. Refunds when a child leaves are built, but the real partner payment call is not connected yet.
  • The app itself. The Parent app has been tested only against practice data and a development server, and it is new. If you find a problem, tell the school.
  • Language. The Parent app shows English only for now.
  • Phone and card readers (NFC). Cards and wristbands depend on the school's tills being set up for them.

11Words used in this manual

Wallet
Your child's pot of money at the school tuck shop. It holds a balance that is added to by you and spent at the till.
Balance
What is left in the wallet right now.
Top-up
Adding money to a wallet, by card, EFT or cash at the school.
EFT
Electronic funds transfer: paying from your bank account. The instant kind confirms in moments.
Link code
The 8 letter code from the school that links you to your child.
Consent
Your agreement that TillKnowIT may keep your child's details so the wallet can work. You give it when you link a child.
Daily limit
The most your child can spend in one day.
Sync
When the till and the app share the latest information. Changes you make reach the till at the next sync.
QR code
A square picture that the cashier scans to take payment from the wallet. It stops working after about a minute.
Cut-off
The last time you may place or cancel a pre-order for a break.
Erasure
Removing your child's name, photo and links, while the money record stays without any name.
