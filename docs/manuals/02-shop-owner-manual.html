1Welcome

You have worked hard for your shop. TillKnowIT is here so that you always know what you have sold, what is on your shelves, what you are owed and what to buy next.

This manual is for you, the owner. It is about the Back office: the part of TillKnowIT where you sit down, usually at a computer or on a phone, and look after the shop. The till is the other part. That is where your staff scan items and take money. The till has its own manual (the next one in this set).

You do not need to be good with computers. If you can use WhatsApp and a banking app, you can use the Back office. Take it one chapter at a time, and keep this manual open next to you while you try things.

How this manual works. Each chapter starts with a short paragraph that says what the screen is for. After that come numbered steps. Words in a green box, like Save product, are the exact words you will see on screen, so you can match them with your eyes. Yellow boxes warn you about common mistakes. Red boxes are for things that lose money or cannot be undone. You do not have to read it in order: use the contents list to jump to what you need. The chapters First day checklist, Words we use and Troubleshooting are at the back.

What the Back office can do

Everything in the left-hand menu is one area of your business. In this manual you will meet all of them:

  • Getting started: make your shop and watch it being verified.
  • Products and stock: see what you have, add and change products.
  • Reordering: see what to buy, compare suppliers, send orders, join group deals.
  • Reports: see how the shop is doing, and download reports.
  • Credit book: see who owes you money.
  • Setup and compliance: the setup checklist and your important documents.
  • Staff and tills: your people and your tills.
  • Business type, Promotions, Modifiers, variants and tables, Services and appointments, Customers and loyalty: extras for the kind of shop you run.
  • Branches: if you own more than one shop.
  • Billing, Accounting, Pay later, Insights and partners, Buying groups: your plan, your accountant, credit, and sharing.

What is live and what is not yet. TillKnowIT is still being finished. We want you to know the truth, so here it is. Text messages (SMS) are not yet being sent through a live service, so some messages described in this manual are tested only against a pretend service for now. Xero and Sage, and the pay-later finance partners, are connected only to test fakes today. The screens in languages other than English are first drafts that no first-language speaker has reviewed yet, and most of the Back office still shows English. Where a chapter touches one of these, it says so in a Note.

Want to try it first? There is a demo owner, Nomsa Dlamini. On the sign-in screen use cell number 082 555 0001. In demo mode the code is shown on the screen under the box, so you do not need a phone. Nothing you do there is real.

2Signing up and signing in

Your cell phone number is your key. There is no password to forget: each time, we send a code to your phone, and the code proves it is you.

If you are new: create an account

Open the Back office in your browser. You will see a green welcome panel and a box called Sign in.

  1. Press I am new here: create an account. A new box appears for your name.
  2. Type your cell number in Your cell phone number. An example is shown: 082 123 4567.
  3. Type your name in Your name. Write it as the people you work with know you.
  4. Press Send me a code. The buttons stay switched off until both boxes are filled in.
  5. You will see: If that number has an account, a code is on its way. Wait for the SMS on your phone.
  6. Type the code in The code we sent you and press Sign in.

You are now signed in. Your name appears at the bottom of the left-hand menu, under Signed in as.

If you already have an account

Leave I am new here: create an account alone. Type your number, press Send me a code, type the code, press Sign in. If you pressed the new-account button by mistake, press I already have an account to go back.

3Creating your shop

Right after signing up you have an account but no shop. The Getting started screen is where you make your shop and then follow how far the setup has got.

Make your shop

The first time, Getting started shows a box called Create your shop. It explains that you will be the owner, and that a TillKnowIT field agent will help you finish setting up and then verify the shop.

  1. Type the name of your shop in Shop name. If you leave it empty you will see: Type the name of your shop.
  2. In Your municipality choose the place where the shop is. You can change this later. The list starts with Choose your municipality.
  3. In Language for the shop choose the language the shop will use.
  4. Press Create my shop.

You will see a green message: your shop name was created. The page then loads again so that your new shop appears in the menu. The Back office now knows which shop you are working for.

Watch your setup

Once the shop exists, Getting started changes. It now shows Your shop's setup.

  • A badge shows the shop's state: Waiting for verification, Verified or Suspended.
  • A line such as 3 of 7 setup steps done shows how far the agent has got.
  • While you wait you will read: A TillKnowIT field agent will visit or call to finish the steps below and verify your shop. You can already add your products and staff while you wait.
  • When all is done: Your shop is verified. Everything is switched on.

4Finding your way around

The Back office has one menu on the left. Press a name and that area opens on the right. This chapter explains the few things that appear again and again.

The left-hand menu

From top to bottom the menu reads: Getting started, Products and stock, Reordering, Reports, Credit book, Branches, Setup and compliance, Staff and tills, Business type, Promotions, Modifiers, variants and tables, Services and appointments, Customers and loyalty, Billing, Accounting, Pay later, Insights and partners and Buying groups. Setup and compliance and Staff and tills may swap places; they are equal in the order.

At the bottom of the menu you will find the Language choice, your name under Signed in as, and Sign out.

Who are you working for?

At the top of each screen is a list called Who are you working for?. It lists every shop (and supplier) you belong to. If you have one shop it chooses that shop for you. If you own several, pick the one you want to look at. Every screen then shows that shop. If you see You are not part of any shop or supplier yet, go to Getting started and create your shop.

There is also a folded section called Advanced: type ids by hand. It is only for testing. Leave it closed.

The box called "Which till is asking"

Some screens (Products and stock, Promotions, Customers and loyalty, Modifiers, variants and tables, Services and appointments, Stock transfers, and Setup and compliance) show a small box called Which till is asking. It reads, for example: Using Till 1, acting as yourself.

This is how the Back office tells the system which approved till and which staff member the change is made for. You choose a Till and a Staff member. Normally you simply act as yourself.

If you have no approved till yet you will see: This shop has no approved till yet. Register and approve one under Staff and tills, then come back. Until then buttons such as Save product stay switched off. Do chapter 10 first. If the box says you have no staff membership at this shop, choose a staff member in the list.

How buttons and messages behave

  • A green message means it worked. For example Chicken feet was saved.
  • A red message means it did not work. It says why in plain words.
  • A grey button with no colour (a "quiet" button) is usually Cancel or Close.
  • Amounts you type are in rand. Write 12.50, not R12,50. The screens show amounts as R.
  • Dates are chosen from a date box.

Who may do what

As the owner you can do everything in this manual. Managers and cashiers have fewer rights, and a few actions (registering and approving tills, choosing the business type, billing and sharing choices) are for the owner only.

5Products and stock

This screen is your shelf, written down. It shows every product, how many you have, and which ones need your attention. It is also where you add a new product or fix a price.

Reading the stock list

Open Products and stock. The first box is called Stock. Use Show to choose a view:

The views you can choose
ViewWhat you see
All productsEverything.
Running lowItems at or below the level you set.
Out of stockItems with none left.
Not sellingItems that have not sold for a while.
ExpiringItems close to their expiry date.

The table has these columns: Product, Barcode, On hand, Low at, Days left (how many days the stock should last at the current pace), Expires, Suggested order, Status and Actions. In Status you may see small badges: Out, Low, Not selling, Expiring, Expired and Needs count. If nothing fits the view you will read No products match this view.

Add a product

Under the table is a box called Add a product.

  1. Type the Product name.
  2. If the item has a barcode, type it or scan it into Barcode (optional). Items sold loose, like a single sweet, may have none.
  3. Choose a Category. The list starts with Choose a category.
  4. Type the Cost price (R): what you pay for one. For example 12.50.
  5. Type the Selling price (R): what the customer pays. For example 18.00.
  6. Type the Low stock level (units): a whole number, 0 or more. When you have this many or fewer, the item shows as Low.
  7. If the item goes off, choose an Expiry date (optional).
  8. Leave Selling this product ticked if you are selling it.
  9. Press Save product. You will see Name was added.

If something is wrong the screen tells you right under the box: Enter the product name, Choose a category, Enter the cost as rands, for example 12.50, Enter the price as rands, for example 18.00, or Enter a whole number of units, 0 or more.

Your cost price is how TillKnowIT works out your profit. If you leave it wrong, your profit figures on the Reports screen will be wrong too. Update the cost when your supplier changes the price.

Edit a product

  1. Find the product in the table and press Edit in its row.
  2. The box below the table changes its title to Edit followed by the product's name, and fills in with the current details. The form is lower down the page. Scroll down if you do not see it.
  3. Change what you need and press Save product. You will see Name was saved.
  4. To stop without changing anything, press Cancel.

Watch the decimal point. A selling price of 180 instead of 18.00 will make the till charge R180. Check the price before you press Save product.

There is no delete button for products. To stop selling something, untick Selling this product and save. The item and its history stay safe, and the till stops offering it. You can tick it again later.

How a product is sold

After you press Edit, a second box appears called How followed by the product name is sold. It changes how the till sells that item. There are three ticks:

Sold by weight
The full words are: Sold by weight (the price is per kilogram and stock is kept in grams). Use it for meat, loose maize meal and similar items. The screen warns that selling by weight needs the Butchery business type (chapter 11). At the till, the cashier weighs the item and the till works out the price.
Ask for a serial number when it is sold
Use this for phones, appliances and tools. The till will ask for the serial number when the item is sold, and the system records it against the sale. It also helps if the customer comes back with a problem.
An extra, only sold together with another item
The full words add "such as a sauce or milk choice". An add-on is not sold alone. It belongs in a modifier group (chapter 13).

Press Save how it is sold. You will see Name will now be sold the way you chose.

Loading many products at once

If you have hundreds of products it would take days to type them in. The system can read an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file, guess which column is which, remember your choice for next time, work out the price per unit from a case price, match items by barcode and then by name, and show you a review table. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

It can also read a photo or PDF of a supplier's invoice, and prepare the stock to receive.

Honest status. The Excel and CSV import is built and tested on the server, but the Back office does not yet have its own screen for it: today the field agent does the first loading of your products for you (the "Your products are loaded" step in chapter 3). The invoice-photo reader is built as far as uploading, queueing, and the review step, but the part that actually reads the picture is not connected yet. A photo you send now will fail with a clear message. Until both are ready, add products one by one with Add a product, or ask your field agent.

Counting and receiving stock

Counting what is on the shelf and receiving a delivery are done at the till, not in the Back office, because that is where the goods are. The till manual shows you how. When you finish a count, the new numbers appear on this screen. An item that is wrong or has not been counted for a long time shows the badge Needs count. You can also confirm a supplier's delivery from the Back office under Your orders (chapter 6).

Be careful with stock counts. A count replaces what the system believes with what the person typed. If a worker counts 4 cartons and types 40, the system will believe you have 40. Choose a careful person, count one shelf at a time, and ask for a manager to check big differences.

6Reordering and group deals

Instead of guessing what to buy, Reordering tells you what is running out, shows what each supplier will charge, and lets you send the orders from here.

The screen has four tabs: Order list, Your orders, Group deals and Suppliers and sharing.

Order list: what to reorder

Under What to reorder you see products that are low. The columns are Product, On hand, Days left, Suggested (how many to order), Best offer and Actions. If nothing is needed you read Nothing needs reordering right now. A product without offers says No offers yet.

  1. Press See offers on a product. A list called Offers for and the product name opens.
  2. Compare the suppliers. The table shows Supplier, Cases, Units received, Total, True cost per unit, Margin, Delivery, Rating and Notes.
  3. Press Choose on the offer you like. It then shows Chosen.
  4. Under How you will pay pick On delivery or In the app.
  5. Tick the products you want to order (the tick is called Add with the product name to the order), then press Build orders. If nothing is chosen you read Choose at least one product above.
  6. You will see N draft orders were built. Open Your orders to send them. One draft is made for each supplier.

Look for the labels on an offer. Cheapest marks the lowest true cost per unit. Usual supplier marks someone you often buy from. Below the supplier's minimum warns that your order is too small for that supplier. Sponsored means the supplier paid to be shown. A sponsored offer is always labelled, and the ranking is built so that, with the normal settings, a sponsored offer never beats a cheaper offer that is not sponsored.

The True cost per unit is worked out for you, including VAT, any deal and the delivery fee, so you compare like with like. Margin shows what you would earn at your current selling price.

Press Make usual supplier on an offer to remember a supplier. You will see Supplier is now a usual supplier.

Your orders

Under Your purchase orders you see each order with Order, Supplier, Status, Payment and Total. An order moves through these states: Draft, Sent, Confirmed, Delivered, and sometimes Cancelled or Disputed. Payment shows Owing, Held, Paid or Refunded. If the supplier changed something you see The supplier changed this order.

  1. On a draft, choose Send by: The app, WhatsApp or Email.
  2. Press Send. You will see Order was sent. To drop a draft press Cancel order.
  3. When the goods arrive, press Confirm delivery. A box called What arrived for order opens. For each line type the cases that really arrived, then press Save what arrived.
  4. Pay. If you chose to pay in the app, press Pay in the app then Continue to the payment page. If you pay the driver in cash, the supplier marks it as paid; you can also press Mark as paid.
  5. Afterwards press Rate to give a Score (1 to 5), and tick It came on time and It was complete if true. Press Send rating. Your honest rating helps other shops.

Count what arrived before you confirm. Your count is the one that wins. If less came than was ordered, type the smaller number, and the system adjusts what you owe.

If something went wrong, press Dispute, write what went wrong in What went wrong and press Raise dispute. You will read: The dispute was raised. The money is held until it is resolved. Money paid in the app is held until you confirm delivery, or until 72 hours pass.

Paying a supplier in the app uses a payment partner. Today only a test stand-in exists for that partner, so paying through the app is not live yet. WhatsApp sending of orders is also tested only against a test stand-in.

Group deals

Suppliers sometimes offer a lower price if many shops order together. Under Group deals near you you see deals in your area: Product, Case price, Progress (for example "40 of 100 cases (40%)"), Closes and Your cases. If there are none you read There are no group deals right now.

  1. In the row, type how many cases you want in Cases for and the product name. Use a whole number, 1 or more.
  2. Press Join. You will see You joined with N cases of product.

Other shops only see the total, never your name or your quantity. When the deal closes, one of two things happens. If enough cases were ordered, one order is sent to the supplier for each shop at the bulk price. If not, everyone is told and nobody is charged.

Suppliers and sharing

Under Sharing your stock levels you choose who may see that your area is running low on something. You choose Share with: Nobody, My usual suppliers or All suppliers, then press Save sharing. Suppliers only ever see counts for shops in your area, never your name or your sales. It is off until you choose.

Under Usual suppliers you see the suppliers you buy from, with the date of your last order. A supplier is added when you press Make usual supplier, or automatically when you receive an order from them. Press Remove to take one off the list. Past orders and prices stay.

7Reports and the owner dashboard

At the end of the day you want to know: did I make money? The Reports screen answers that, and lets you take the numbers to your accountant.

The owner dashboard: "How the shop is doing"

The top box of Reports is your dashboard. It is the first thing to look at each morning. The first table has three rows, Today, This week and This month, with these columns:

Sales
All the money customers paid.
Profit
Sales minus what the goods cost you.
Customers
How many customers bought.
Average sale
Sales divided by the number of customers.

Below that are four lists:

  • Best sellers: Product, Units sold, Revenue, Profit.
  • Running low: items to buy soon, with On hand and Days left.
  • Not selling: items sitting on the shelf. They tie up your money.

An empty list says Nothing to show. That is good news.

  4. In Shops to include choose This shop. If you have branches you can also choose All shops in the business or one branch.
  5. Press Download report. The button says Preparing the report... for a moment, then you read Your report file name was downloaded. Look in your Downloads folder.

8The credit book

Many customers ask for "a book". The Credit book shows who you have given credit to and what each person owes, so nothing is forgotten.

The screen is for looking. Credit sales and payments are taken at the till; here you see the result. The box is called Customers on credit and shows Customer, Phone, Credit limit, Owes, Status and Actions. A person with no limit shows No limit. Status reads Within limit or Over limit. Under the table you read the total: Total owed: R...

  1. Press View entries next to a name.
  2. A box called Entries for and the customer's name opens. Each line has Date, Type, Amount and Paid by.
  3. The type is Credit sale, Payment or Cancelled sale.
  4. Press Close when you are done.

If customers are over their limit you see: N customers are over their credit limit. This is only a warning: sales are never blocked. The system will not stop a cashier selling on credit. You must decide yourself whether to say no.

Download the Credit book report (chapter 7) at month end. It is a handy list to take when you go to collect.

9Setup checklist and compliance documents

Two things live under Setup and compliance: the list of setup steps, and a safe place for your important papers.

The screen has two tabs: Setup checklist and Compliance documents.

Setup checklist

The tab shows Shop setup. The hint explains that these steps are normally completed by the TillKnowIT field agent, and that the signed-in person is recorded as the agent. The table has Step, Done, Note and Actions. The steps are: Device check, Shop details, Document photos, Catalogue import, Staff PINs, Test sale and Training sign-off. At the top you read Shop status followed by the state.

  1. Press Mark done on a step that is really finished. You will see Step was marked as done.
  2. When all are finished you read Every step is done.

Do not mark a step done to make the list look finished. The shop is verified by the field agent. A step that is marked but not truly done will cause trouble later.

Compliance documents

Spaza and tuckshops often need papers: a business registration, a health certificate, a trading licence and so on. This tab keeps them in one safe place and reminds you before they run out. They are stored locked (encrypted) and only you and the people allowed can open them.

The Documents box shows each document with Document, File, Expires and Status. The status is Valid, Expiring soon or Expired, with a line like 25 days left or No expiry. If there are none you read No documents uploaded yet.

Upload a document

  1. In Upload a document choose the Document type from the list. If you forget you read Choose what kind of document this is.
  2. Press to choose the File. It must be a photo (JPEG or PNG) or a PDF, up to 10 MB. Without a file you read Choose a file to upload.
  3. If the paper has an end date, choose it in Expiry date (optional).
  4. Press Upload document. You will see File was uploaded.

To see a document again press Download. To remove one press Delete.

Delete removes the document. Make sure you still have the original paper before you press it.

When a document gets close to its end date, TillKnowIT sends a reminder at 30 days, at 7 days, and on the day it expires. Each reminder is sent once. Reminders are meant to go by WhatsApp or email.

The WhatsApp and email services that send these reminders are not connected to a live provider yet, so for now watch the Status column yourself. Also, the files are kept on an encrypted disk of our own for now.

10Staff and tills

Your staff are the people who sell, and your tills are the devices they sell on. This screen controls who may use which till. It protects your money.

Add a staff member

The top box is called Staff. The hint says it lists everyone who works at this shop, and that the staff id is the one tills and selling screens use.

  1. Type the Name and the Phone number.
  2. Choose the Role: Cashier or Manager. A manager can approve things a cashier cannot, such as a big refund or a price change.
  3. Choose a PIN (4 to 6 digits). This is what they type on the till to unlock it.
  4. Press Add staff member. You will see Name was added. Their staff id is ... The long staff id is needed in some other screens (for example for services), so you can copy it from the table later.

The table shows Name, Role, Status (Active or Disabled), Staff id and Actions. You will see No staff yet. before you add anyone. You, the owner, appear with the role Owner.

Change a PIN, or stop someone working

  1. In the person's row, type a new number in New PIN for and the name. A PIN is 4 to 6 digits, or you read A PIN is 4 to 6 digits.
  2. Press Change PIN for and the name. You will see The PIN for name was changed.
  3. To stop someone from using any till, press Disable and the name. Their status becomes Disabled. To let them work again press Enable.

Disable, do not just ignore, a person who has left. Also change the PIN when a staff member shares theirs or if you think someone saw it. On the till, five wrong PIN tries lock it for five minutes.

Register a till

Every device that sells must be registered and approved by you first. This stops a stranger from putting TillKnowIT on a phone and selling in your name. The second box is called Tills. It lists every till for the shop with Till, Status, Last seen and Actions.

  1. Type a name in Till name (for example "Front counter") and press Register till. You will see Name was registered. Approve it before it can sell.
  2. A box opens called Set up the till and the name. It shows the Shop id, the Device id and the Setup code (type it into the till).
  3. Write the setup code down now. It is shown only once.
  4. In the row press Approve and the till name. The status changes from Waiting for approval to Approved.
  5. On the till, enter the setup code together with the shop id and the till id the first time it is set up. The till manual walks through that screen.

The setup code is shown once and cannot be shown again. If you lose it you must reset the till (below) to get a new one.

The setup panel also shows a value for the browser's tillknowit.device storage. This is a technical step. Your field agent usually does it for you the first time. After that the till proves who it is with a signed code that lasts 30 days.

Press Show setup for and the till name at any time to see the shop id and device id again (but not the code).

Reset a till

Use this when a till lost its saved sign-in, was reinstalled, or you think someone else has it.

  1. Press Reset and the till name. A yellow warning explains what will happen.
  2. Press Yes, reset and the till name (or Keep to change your mind).
  3. You read Name was reset. Type the new setup code into the till, then approve it. Write the new code down, type it into the till and press Approve again.

A reset stops the till's old credential from working and the till must be approved again. Sales that were already waiting on the till are kept.

Wipe a lost till

If a till is lost or stolen, press Wipe and the till name, then Yes, wipe and the name. The status shows Wipe requested. You read: Wiping removes the shop data from that till the next time it connects. If you press Keep nothing happens.

Wiping removes the shop's data from that device the next time it is online. Only do it for a till you really want to clear. A till that is switched off or never goes online will still hold its data until it connects.

11Business type

A spaza shop, a cafe and a hair salon do not need the same till. Your Business type decides which extras your tills show, so that staff are not buried in buttons they never use.

The box Your shop is on shows the type you have now, with its Version. If only the basic till is on you read No features beyond the basic till are switched on. Below, Choose what kind of business this is lists the types you can pick. Each one has a list called Switches on. A basic type says Nothing extra: the basic till.

The types we supply today are the basic spaza till and packs for a cafe, a butchery, a salon, and clothing and hardware. The exact list on your screen is the one that counts.

  1. Read the Switches on list of each type.
  2. Press Choose followed by the type's name on the one that suits you. The one you use is marked Current.
  3. You will see Your shop now uses name. Tills pick up the change the next time they are online.

The extras that a type can switch on are: Split payments, Refunds, Open tickets, Tables, Kitchen screen, Tips, Product variants, Modifiers, Promotions, Selling by weight, Price in the barcode, Appointments, Commission, Quotes, Lay-by, Serial numbers, Customers, Loyalty points, Gift vouchers, Receipt printer, Cash drawer, Scale and Message bundles.

It is safe to switch. Nothing is lost: no product, sale or customer is touched. If you switch back, everything you had before returns. Only the owner can do this.

If a screen in the Back office says a part is "not switched on" for your shop, it is waiting for the right business type. For example Selling by weight needs the butchery type and Appointments needs the salon type.

12Promotions

A special, such as "2 for R30" or "10% off on Fridays", brings people in. Promotions lets you set one up once, and the till applies it by itself.

The list Your promotions shows Promotion, Kind, Deal, When, Status and Actions. A promotion is On or Off. Press Turn on or Turn off and the name to switch it. Press Edit and the name to change it.

The four kinds

Percent off
A percentage off the price. Enter a number from 1 to 100.
Amount off
A rand amount taken off.
Fixed price
The product sells at a set price while the promotion is on.
Combo
A bundle: for example 3 items for R50. You list the items and quantities.

Add a promotion

  1. Under Add a promotion type a Name that you will recognise.
  2. Choose the Kind of promotion.
  3. Fill in Percent off (1 to 100), or Amount in rand, or Fixed price in rand, or Combo price in rand, depending on the kind. A bad amount shows Enter an amount like 12.50.
  4. Choose the First day and Last day if the special has an end.
  5. Under Days (none ticked means every day) tick the days of the week, Mon to Sun.
  6. If it only runs at certain times, fill in From (hour, optional) and To (hour, optional), in local time like 16:00. Leave both empty for the whole day.
  7. Under Products it applies to tick the products. For a combo use Combo items: choose the Combo product, the Quantity and press Add combo item.
  8. Press Save promotion. You will see Name was saved. To stop editing press Stop editing.

Promotions do not stack: one product gets one promotion at a time. Hours are South African time, and they can run past midnight.

Try a basket

Before you let customers meet a promotion, test it. The box Try a basket prices a pretend basket the way the till will, using the promotions that are on.

  1. Choose a Product, a Quantity and the Unit price in rand, then press Add line for each item.
  2. Press Work out the price.
  3. Read the Subtotal, Discount and Total, and which promotions were used under Promotions used. If none matched you read No promotion applied.

Check your margin before you launch. A "fixed price" below your cost price means you lose money on every sale. Look at the product's cost in Products and stock first.

13Modifiers, variants and tables

These are for shops that sell the same thing in different ways: a burger with or without cheese, a T-shirt in three sizes, a table in a restaurant. The screen has three tabs: Modifier groups, Variants and Floor plan.

Modifier groups

A modifier group is a set of extras a customer picks from, like sauces or milk types. Each extra is a product in your shop, so it has its own price and its own stock. (Make each extra a product first, and tick An extra, only sold together with another item in chapter 5.)

  1. Under Add a modifier group type a Group name (for example "Sauces").
  2. Tick The customer must choose if a choice is compulsory.
  3. Set Fewest choices and Most choices.
  4. Under Extras in this group tick the products that belong.
  5. Press Save group. You will see Name was saved.

The list shows a rule such as Pick 1 to 1 or Optional, up to 2. To change a group press Edit and the name, then Stop editing if you change your mind.

Then connect groups to products. Under Put groups on a product choose the Product, tick the Groups this product offers and press Save groups for this product. If you have not made a group yet you read Make a modifier group first.

Variants

Variants are sizes or flavours of one product, each with its own barcode, price and stock. Under Variants of a product choose the Product first (otherwise: Choose a product first.). Then fill in Variant label (for example Large), Variant barcode (optional), Cost in rand and Price in rand and press Add variant. The table lists Label, Barcode, Cost and Price. Labels and barcodes cannot repeat for one product.

Floor plan

For cafes and restaurants. Here you list your tables so the till can put an order on a table. Press Add table and fill in Area (for example "Patio"), Table (the name or number) and Seats. Press Remove and the name to take one out. Then press Save floor plan. You will see The floor plan was saved (N tables).

Saving replaces the whole plan with the tables you see on screen. A table with an open ticket is best left alone: finish or close the ticket at the till before you remove it. Every table needs a name and at least one seat.

14Services and appointments

If you cut hair, braai for functions or repair phones, you sell time, not just goods. This area handles the services you offer, who booked what, and what your staff earn in commission. It needs the salon business type.

The three tabs are Services, Appointments and Commission.

Services

Services you offer lists Service, Minutes, Price, Staff who do it and Offered.

  1. Under Add a service type the Service name.
  2. Fill in Duration in minutes and Price in rand (for example 150 or 150.50).
  3. In Staff ids who do it paste the staff ids, separated by commas. Leave it empty if anyone can do it (it then shows Anyone). Copy the ids from the table on Staff and tills.
  4. Leave Offered to customers ticked if you are taking bookings.
  5. Press Save service.

Appointments

Choose the Day and you see Appointments on that day: Time, Customer, Provider, Status. If two bookings overlap, a badge says Clashes with another booking. Each booking can be Booked, Confirmed, Done, Cancelled or No-show. The buttons on a row are Confirm, Complete, Cancel, No-show and Reschedule.

  1. Under Book an appointment choose the Service.
  2. Fill in Provider staff id, Customer name, optionally Customer phone (optional), Start time and Notes (optional).
  3. Press Book it. You will see Booked name for time.

To move a booking press Reschedule, choose the new time under New time for, and press Save new time for. Press Keep the old time to back out.

If a customer agreed to be messaged, TillKnowIT is built to send a reminder 24 hours before the appointment, by WhatsApp. The WhatsApp provider is not live yet, and reminders are counted against your message bundle (chapter 17).

Commission

A commission rule gives a percentage of a service's price to the staff member who did it. Under Commission rules choose Staff id (optional), Service (optional) and Percent (0 to 100), then press Save rule. Leave staff or service empty for a rule that covers everyone or every service (the table then shows All staff or All services). The most specific rule wins.

Under Commission report choose From, To and Format and press Download report.

15Customers, vouchers, quotes and lay-by

Your regular customers are your shop's best friends. Customers and loyalty helps you remember them, reward them, and handle gift vouchers, price quotes and lay-by.

The four tabs are Customers, Gift vouchers, Quotes and Lay-by.

Customers

The system only lists customers that match a search. Under Find a customer type a name or a phone number in Name or phone number and press Find. The table shows Name, Phone, Email, May be messaged and Points. If nobody matches you read No customer matches that.

Add a customer

Under Add a customer fill in Name, Phone number and Email (optional). Tick The customer agreed to be sent messages only if they said yes. Press Add customer.

Only tick the message box when the customer has really agreed. That is the law (POPIA), and it is also good manners.

Open a customer

Press Open and the name. You see Customer and the name, and can press Save changes after editing. The Points box shows the balance, for example 120 points, worth R12.00, and the minimum needed to redeem. The History box lists When, Kind, Points and Note.

  • Redeem points. Type Points to redeem and press Redeem points. You get a reference number. Give that value off at the till.
  • Adjust points. Type Points to add (or take away with a minus), a Reason, and the Manager's staff id (approves the change), then press Adjust points.
  • Merge a duplicate. If the same person is in the list twice, open the one to keep, choose the Duplicate to merge in and press Merge into and the name. The duplicate must appear in the list above, so search again if you cannot find it.

A merge cannot be undone. Points and history move to the customer you kept, and the duplicate stops being used. Check both names twice.

Gift vouchers

A voucher lets someone buy a gift for another person, or lets you refund as credit.

  1. Under Sell a gift voucher type the Amount in rand.
  2. Choose how it was paid in Paid with: Cash, Card or QR.
  3. Press Create voucher.
  4. Write the code down now or give it to the customer. It reads Voucher code: ... and is shown only once. Afterwards only the last four characters are known.

To check a voucher type the Voucher code and press Check balance. You may read Voucher ending 1234: balance R150.00, or This voucher is void, or This voucher has expired. To take money off a voucher, type the Amount to take off the voucher, in rand and press Use voucher. Using a voucher needs an internet connection.

Quotes

A quote is a price you promise for a few days: useful for hardware or school uniform orders. The list shows Number, Customer, Total, Valid until and Status.

  1. Under Make a quote fill in Quote number, and optionally the customer's name and phone.
  2. Choose the date in Valid until. A quote can be valid for up to 90 days.
  3. Add lines: choose a Product, Quantity, Unit price in rand and press Add line. You need at least one line.
  4. Press Save quote.

Press Open PDF of and the number to give the customer a printed quote. When the customer says yes, press Make the number a sale. You read The quote became a ticket. Finish the sale at the till. The money is taken at the till. Press Cancel and the number to cancel the quote.

Lay-by

Lay-by lets a customer pay for something in parts while you keep it aside. The screen explains: lay-bys are started at the till. Here you take payments, hand over the goods or cancel. The table shows Reference, Customer, Total, Paid, Balance, Due and Status.

  1. To take a payment type the amount in Payment for the reference, choose Paid with for the reference and press Take payment for. You read Payment taken. The reference has R... to pay.
  2. When it is paid up, type the sale id in Sale id to collect and press Collect the reference.
  3. To cancel press Cancel and the reference. You read the cancellation fee and the refund due.

The box Stock held for lay-bys shows the products being kept aside (Product and Held).

A cancelled lay-by costs the customer a fee, set by your business type. Make sure the customer knows this when they start one.

16Branches and stock transfers

If you have more than one shop, one is the head shop and the others are branches. This area keeps them together, so you can see all of them in one place.

The tabs are Shops, Manager access and Stock transfers.

Branches are part of the Pro plan. If you are on a plan without it, you read Branches are part of the Pro plan. See chapter 17.

Shops

Your shops lists Shop, Kind (Head shop or Branch), Area and Status. To add one, under Add a branch type the Branch name, optionally a Municipality code (optional) (empty means the head shop's area) and press Add branch. A branch sits under the head shop; there is only one level.

Manager access

Under Give a manager access to all branches, the person you choose can then see and manage every branch, not just one. Type the Person id of the manager and press Give access. You read That person can now manage all branches. Staff who work in only one branch still see only that one.

Stock transfers

When one shop has a lot of an item and another is short, move it. Stock transfers shows each move: From, To, Status, Sent, Items. Use Show to filter All transfers, In transit, Received or Cancelled.

  1. Under Send stock to a branch choose where in Send to (the list starts with Choose...).
  2. Add lines: Product and Quantity, then Add item.
  3. Optionally add a Note (optional), then press Send transfer. The stock leaves your shop at once.
  4. At the other end, press Receive. The box How many of each item arrived? opens. Type the real number for each item and press Confirm received. If fewer came, the shortfall shows as Short by a number.
  5. While it is still In transit you can press Cancel transfer.

Count what arrives before you type the numbers. The stock on the sending shop went down the moment you pressed send, so a wrong "arrived" number makes both shops wrong.

Reports can cover all your branches. In Reports, choose All shops in the business under Shops to include. Products with the same name are merged across branches.

Transfers need an internet connection. Sending them while a till is offline is not built yet.

17Billing

The basic till is free. Some extras are part of a paid plan. Billing is where you see your plan, choose another, and pay invoices.

Selling at the till is never affected by billing. If a payment fails, only the extra features of a paid plan can pause. Your till keeps selling.

Your plan

The box Your plan shows Plan, Current period ends and the status: Active, Payment overdue, Limited or Cancelled. If you have no plan you read You are on the free plan. Choose a plan below to add more features. Banners may also appear:

  • We could not take your last payment. Your paid features keep working for now. Pay the invoice below to stay on your plan.
  • Your paid features are paused because the payment is overdue. Pay the invoice below to switch them back on.
  • Your plan ends on date. Until then everything keeps working.
  • You move to plan on date.

Choose or change a plan

Plans lists what is on offer, each priced as "R... a month" or Free, with what it includes. The features you may meet are: Messages to customers, Several branches, Accounting export, Insight reports, Pay later, Partner data access, Sponsored placements and Supplier analytics. Each shows included or up to a number.

  1. Read the plan you want.
  2. Press Subscribe to, Upgrade to or Downgrade to and the plan name.
  3. An upgrade takes effect now and you pay only the difference for the rest of the month. If your payment is declined, nothing changes. A downgrade or cancel happens at the end of the period.
  4. To leave, press Cancel plan. You read Your plan will end at the end of this period.

Invoices

Invoices lists Invoice, Status (Open, Paid, Payment failed, Void), Total, Due and Paid. Press View and the number to see the lines (Description, Quantity, Unit price, Amount) with Subtotal, VAT and Total. Press Pay and the number to pay it.

The prices you see for plans are placeholders until the final pricing decision, and the real payment gateway is not connected yet, so a test charger stands in. The free message bundle on a free plan is 50 messages a month as a placeholder. When a free bundle runs out, slips turn into a QR code and reminders pause; a paid plan keeps sending and bills the extra. You are told at 80% and when it is used up.

18Accounting: Xero and Sage

If you or your bookkeeper use Xero or Sage, TillKnowIT can send the shop's takings every night, so the books stay up to date without typing. This is part of a paid plan: see Billing.

Not live yet. The Xero and Sage connections are written to each package's documentation and tested only against pretend servers. Nothing has been run against a real Xero or Sage account yet. Treat this chapter as a preview and ask your TillKnowIT contact before you rely on it.

Connect your package

  1. Open Accounting. Under Connect your accounting package choose the Accounting package: Xero or Sage.
  2. Press Sign in with and the package name. You sign in on the package's own page and come back here. You then read: You are connected. Choose your accounts below, then your days are sent every night.
  3. If your set-up gave you an authorisation code instead, paste it into Authorisation code and press Connect.
  4. Optionally choose First day to send (optional). Days before it are not sent. Empty means start today.

Once connected you read Connected to Xero. You can press Disconnect, or connect again to replace it (Replace connection).

Match each kind of money to an account

Under Which account does each kind of money go to? type the account code from your own books for each line. All nine are needed before a day can be sent:

  • Cash takings
  • Card and QR clearing
  • Customers who owe you (credit book)
  • Other receipts
  • Sales (excluding VAT)
  • VAT on sales
  • Cost of sales
  • Stock
  • Owed to suppliers

Press Save accounts. If something is missing you read Still needed: followed by the list. Ask your bookkeeper for the codes. Do not guess.

Check what was sent

Sent to your books lists each finished day: Day, Package, Status (Waiting, Sent, Failed), Tries, Reference and Problem. The system retries a failed day every hour, up to five times. After that you read We stopped trying. Fix the problem, then retry. Fix the cause (often a wrong account code), then press Retry and the day. Sending a day twice does not post it twice.

Tips and lay-by deposits are not yet included in the journal.

19Pay later

Sometimes the supplier wants money today but your customers have not paid you yet. A finance partner may lend you the money to pay your supplier, and you repay later. TillKnowIT only makes the introduction. We never lend you money ourselves: the partner decides.

Not live yet. No real finance partner is connected today and there is no agreement in place. Only a test partner exists. This chapter shows how it will work. A legal review is still needed before any shop is asked to share data. Pay later is part of a paid plan.

How your information is used

The screen explains it in a box called How pay later works. To decide, the partner needs to see your sales. When you say yes, only your total sales for each of the last months you choose are shared. Never what you sold, who bought it, or your customers. You can say no at any time, and every share is written in a log.

Agree to share

  1. Under Share your sales with a finance partner choose the Finance partner to share with.
  2. Type Months of sales to share (1 to 24).
  3. Press I agree to share my sales. You read You agreed to share your sales with partner.

Your agreements lists Partner, Months shared, Agreed on and State. To stop, press Withdraw agreement with and the partner. Nothing more is shared from then on.

Apply, and pay an order with credit

  1. Under Apply for credit choose the partner, type How much credit do you want (rand)? (like 5000 or 5000.50) and press Apply. Without an agreement you read Agree to share your sales with a partner first.
  2. Your applications shows Waiting for the partner, Approved or Declined, with Credit limit, Still available and Terms.
  3. Once approved, under Pay a supplier order on credit terms choose the Approved credit and the Supplier order and press Pay the order with credit. You read The supplier is paid. You repay the partner by date.
  4. Watch Repayments: Supplier, Paid for you, To repay, Repaid so far, Due.

Credit is a loan. You must repay it by the due date. Only borrow what you know you can pay back from your takings.

The box What was shared about you shows every time something was shared and what it was. Repayments are reported to us by the partner, so they can take a little while to show.

20Insights and partner access

Your sales can teach others, and others can help you, but only if you say so. This screen is where you decide. It is off until you switch it on.

This is part of a paid plan. A legal and data-ethics review is still required before any shop is asked to opt in, so treat it as a preview.

Market insights

Under Market insights there is one tick: Include my shop in market insights. The screen shows Your shop is included or not included, and when you last changed it. Press it, and you read Your choice was saved.

When you opt in, your shop's totals are joined with those of many other shops to make area trend reports. The report has no shop name, person or till on it, and an area with fewer than 10 shops is never shown. Only the owner can choose.

Partner access to your sales

A partner (for example an accounting or finance firm) can read your sales figures, but only the kinds of data you approve, and you can withdraw at any time.

  1. Under Approve a partner type the Partner client id. The partner gives you this id; partners are registered by TillKnowIT.
  2. Under What the partner may read tick Daily sales totals and/or Monthly sales totals. You must choose at least one.
  3. Press Approve access. You read partner can now read what you approved.

Approved partners lists Partner, May read, Approved on and State. Press Withdraw access for and the partner to stop it. Every call a partner makes is logged.

21Buying groups

A stokvel, a group of nearby tuckshops or a school can buy together, so the supplier gives everyone the bulk price. A Buying group is that club.

Your buying groups lists Group, Kind (Stokvel, School or Other), Shops and Your role (Group admin or Member). If you are in none you read Your shop is not in a buying group yet.

Start a group

  1. Under Start a group type the Group name and choose the Kind of group.
  2. Press Create group. You read Name was created. You become the group admin.

Invite a shop

  1. On your group's row press Invite a shop to and the group name.
  2. A code appears: Code: ... Share it with the shop owner you want to invite (for example on WhatsApp).
  3. The code works once, lasts seven days, and is shown only now. Write it down or send it straight away.

Join a group

Under Join a group with a code type the Invite code and press Join group. You read You joined name.

History, members and leaving

Press History of and the group name to see Order history of the group: Product, Supplier, Status, Cases so far, Your cases and Closes. The group admin also sees Members (Shop, Joined, Cases on open deals) and can press Remove and the name. Others read Only the group admin sees the members. To leave, press Leave and the group name.

Buying groups are built on the server and the Back office has the screen, but the screen has not yet been used with real shops. If something looks odd, tell us.

22First day checklist

Print this page and tick as you go. If you do these in order, you will be ready to sell by the end of your first day.

Before the field agent arrives

  1. Sign up with your name and cell number, and sign in with the code (chapter 2).
  2. Create your shop: name, municipality and language (chapter 3).
  3. Choose your business type (chapter 11).
  4. Collect your documents and any papers that expire (chapter 9). Have the files or clear photos ready.
  5. List your staff with their cell numbers and decide each person's role and PIN.

With the field agent (or on your own)

  1. Add your products, or let the agent load them (chapter 5). Check cost and selling prices.
  2. Set a low stock level on your fast sellers.
  3. Add your staff and give each a PIN (chapter 10).
  4. Register your till, write down the setup code, approve it, and type the code into the till.
  5. Do a test sale with a cashier. Cancel it afterwards.
  6. Upload your compliance documents (chapter 9).

  2. Check Running low in Reordering and decide what to buy tomorrow.
  3. Download Sales by day as PDF to see what a report looks like.
  4. Make sure nobody shares a PIN. Sign out of the Back office.

Keep the setup code, the voucher codes and your tills' names on paper, in a safe place. The system does not show those codes twice.

23Words we use

These are the words you will meet most often, in plain language.

Add-on
An extra that is only sold together with another item, such as a sauce or a milk choice.
Back office
The part of TillKnowIT you use at a desk or on your phone to run the shop. Not the till.
Branch
A shop of yours that sits under your head shop.
Business type
The kind of shop you run (basic spaza, cafe, butchery, salon, clothing and hardware). It decides which extras the till shows.
Buying group
Shops that buy together so the supplier gives a bulk price.
Case
A box or carton of items as the supplier sells them.
Cash-up
Counting the money in a till at the end of a shift or day and checking it against what the system expected.
Compliance documents
The papers that show your shop is allowed to trade, such as licences and certificates.
Credit book
The list of customers who bought on credit and what they still owe.
CSV
A simple file that Excel can open. TillKnowIT offers it as CSV (opens in Excel).
Field agent
A TillKnowIT person who helps you finish setup and then verifies your shop.
Group deal
A supplier's special price that is offered if enough shops order together.
Head shop
The main shop that branches belong to.
Lay-by
You keep goods aside while the customer pays in parts. They collect when it is paid.
Low stock level
The number at or below which an item shows as running low.
Modifier group
A set of extras the customer picks from, such as sauces.
OTP / one-time code
The code sent to your phone to prove it is you. It works once.
PIN
A number of 4 to 6 digits a staff member types to unlock the till.
Setup code
The code shown once when you register a till. You type it into the till to connect it.
Slip
A receipt for a customer, printed or sent.
Sponsored
An offer that a supplier paid to show. It is always labelled.
Staff id
A long code that names one staff member inside the system. You copy it into other screens.
Till
The device where your staff sell. Each one must be registered and approved by you.
Variant
A size or flavour of one product, with its own barcode and price.
Verified
The state of a shop that the field agent has checked and approved.
Wipe
Ask a till to clear the shop's data from itself, used for a lost till.
