●Printing and saving as PDF

Open a manual in a browser and choose Print, then Save as PDF. Pages are set for A4, with the TillKnowIT logo and the manual's name at the top of every sheet and the contents on a page of their own. Turn on Background graphics so the green cover prints with its colour.
